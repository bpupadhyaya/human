## What this platform is

**VLP (Virus-Like Particle) vaccines** and related **recombinant protein-subunit vaccines** present viral structural proteins that self-assemble into particles morphologically similar to the native virus, but without any genetic material — they cannot replicate or cause infection. The structural mimicry of the native virion surface is key: multivalent, geometrically repetitive antigen display activates B cells more effectively than monomeric antigen alone.

The **VHP (Virus-like particle, Hepatitis B surface antigen, Protein-subunit)** platform designation covers:
- **Virus-like particles (VLPs)**: proteins that self-assemble into icosahedral or tubular particles identical to the virion capsid/envelope but lacking nucleic acid (Gardasil, Cervarix, Engerix-B)
- **Recombinant protein subunits**: individual structural proteins produced in heterologous expression systems (Hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg), Novavax NVX-CoV2373 spike nanoparticles)
