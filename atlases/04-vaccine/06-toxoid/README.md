## What this platform is

A toxoid vaccine delivers a **chemically inactivated form of a bacterial exotoxin** — a protein toxin whose toxic activity has been destroyed (by formaldehyde treatment) while its **immunogenicity is preserved**. The immune system generates neutralizing antibodies against the toxoid that also recognize and neutralize the native toxin, providing protection before any disease can develop.

Toxoid vaccines are among the oldest and most successful vaccines in history. Tetanus toxoid has been in use since the 1920s; diphtheria toxoid since the 1930s. They have near-zero incidence of the diseases they prevent in vaccinated populations.

---

## Why toxoids work for toxin-mediated disease

Some of the most lethal bacterial infections kill not through bacterial invasion of deep tissues, but through **exotoxins** — protein toxins secreted into the host. Tetanus toxin and diphtheria toxin are paradigmatic examples: even a small amount of free toxin is lethal. Vaccination with the inactivated toxin trains the immune system to produce **neutralizing antibodies** that intercept the toxin before it reaches its target. No toxin → no disease, even if bacteria colonize superficially.
