## What this platform is

An inactivated vaccine contains **whole pathogen particles (virions, bacteria, or parasites) rendered non-replicative by chemical or physical treatment** — most commonly beta-propiolactone (BPL), formaldehyde, or heat — while preserving enough surface and structural antigen integrity to elicit an immune response. The pathogen cannot replicate in the host. This is one of the oldest and most widely deployed vaccine technologies; inactivated influenza, polio (Salk IPV), and hepatitis A vaccines use this approach.

Key characteristics of the platform:

- **Whole-pathogen antigen repertoire** — presents spike/surface, nucleoprotein, membrane, and other antigens simultaneously; broader antigen coverage than subunit or mRNA approaches, potentially reducing pressure for variant escape.
- **No replication** — safe in immunocompromised individuals (unlike live-attenuated vaccines); no risk of reversion to virulence.
- **Aluminum-based adjuvants common** — aluminum hydroxide or aluminum phosphate is added to boost immunogenicity; Th2-skewed humoral response is characteristic.
- **Traditional manufacturing** — large-scale cell culture (Vero cells, egg-based, or bacterial culture), inactivation, and purification; well-understood at global manufacturing scale.
- **Modest immunogenicity relative to replication-competent platforms** — usually requires multi-dose primary series and periodic boosters; peak neutralizing antibody titers typically lower than mRNA or live-attenuated platforms.
- **Cold chain: 2–8°C** — refrigerator-stable; no ultra-cold requirements; strong supply-chain advantage for low- and middle-income countries.

## Common architecture

| Component | Role |
|:---|:---|
| **Inactivated pathogen particle** | Intact (or disrupted) virion / bacterium / parasite presenting authentic surface antigens |
| **Inactivation agent** | BPL, formaldehyde, or heat; destroys replicative nucleic acid while preserving antigenicity |
| **Adjuvant (usually alum)** | Depot effect at injection site; NLRP3 inflammasome activation; promotes innate sensing and antigen-presenting-cell maturation |
| **Buffer / stabilizers** | Phosphate buffer, sucrose, gelatin, or other stabilizers to maintain potency during storage |
