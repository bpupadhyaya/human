# FH

## Overview

**FH** (fumarate hydratase; also fumarase) encodes a 510-amino-acid (54 kDa) enzyme that catalyzes the reversible hydration of fumarate to L-malate in the **tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle**. FH exists as two isoforms from the same gene: a longer mitochondrial isoform (FH2; N-terminal mitochondrial targeting sequence) that participates in TCA cycle oxidative metabolism, and a shorter cytosolic isoform (FH1; generated by alternative translation initiation) involved in the argininosuccinate lyase reaction in the urea cycle and DNA damage repair at double-strand breaks. FH is a classical tumor suppressor: germline pathogenic FH variants cause **hereditary leiomyomatosis and renal cell carcinoma (HLRCC)**, an autosomal dominant syndrome featuring cutaneous and uterine leiomyomas plus highly aggressive FH-deficient RCC. Biallelic FH inactivation causes profound fumarate accumulation — fumarate is a potent competitive inhibitor of α-ketoglutarate (2-oxoglutarate)-dependent dioxygenases including **TET2** (DNA demethylase), **histone KDMs**, and **PHD1/2/3** (prolyl hydroxylase domain enzymes) — creating a pseudohypoxic, epigenetically reprogrammed state [^tomlinson-2002-fh] [^linehan-2013-fh-review].

**FH in the oncometabolite landscape:**

| Oncometabolite | Gene LOF | Enzyme inhibited | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2-hydroxyglutarate (2-HG) | IDH1/IDH2 mutation | TET2, KDMs, PHD | G-CIMP, pseudohypoxia, EZH2 dependence |
| Fumarate | FH LOF | TET2, KDMs, PHD | CIMP-like epigenome, pseudohypoxia, KEAP1 succination |
| Succinate | SDHB/C/D LOF | TET2, KDMs, PHD | DNA hypermethylation, pseudohypoxia, PHEO/PGL |
| Common mechanism | All three | α-KG-dependent dioxygenases | Epigenetic + metabolic reprogramming |

## Structure

### FH protein architecture

**Mitochondrial isoform (FH2; full-length ~511 aa pre-form):**
N-terminal mitochondrial targeting sequence (MTS, aa 1-43) → cleaved upon mitochondrial import → mature FH (~470 aa); tetrameric enzyme (four identical subunits of 467 aa each); TCA cycle localization: inner mitochondrial matrix; catalyzes fumarate + H2O ↔ L-malate; reaction is the 7th step of TCA cycle (after succinate dehydrogenase/SDHB complex); Km for fumarate: ~100 μM; kcat: ~1,200 s⁻¹

**Cytosolic isoform (FH1; shorter form):**
Generated by alternative start codon (Met23 or Met30) → lacks MTS → cytosolic; same catalytic domain as FH2; participates in argininosuccinate lyase reaction in cytoplasm; role in cytosolic fumarate metabolism; newly recognized role: recruited to DNA double-strand breaks (DSBs) → local fumarate production → inhibits KDM2A (histone H3K36 demethylase) → increases H3K36me2 at DSB loci → promotes NHEJ over HR; FH1 at DSBs is a normal DNA repair function exploited in FH-deficient tumors

**Active site:**
His-188, His-351, Lys-324 constitute the catalytic triad; fumarate binds in a linear conformation; tetrameric FH has four active sites at subunit interfaces; missense pathogenic variants (R190H, E319K) directly disrupt the active site or subunit interface; truncating variants produce unstable protein degraded by the proteasome

**IHC markers for FH deficiency:**
Two complementary IHC markers:
1. **FH IHC (anti-FH antibody)**: loss of FH protein (nuclear + cytoplasmic) in tumor cells; internal control: stromal cells, vascular endothelium retain FH staining; sensitivity ~85-90% for biallelic FH LOF
2. **2SC IHC (anti-S-(2-succinyl)cysteine antibody)**: fumarate accumulation → non-enzymatic covalent modification (succination) of cysteine residues on proteins → 2SC neoepitope detected by anti-2SC antibody; positive 2SC (cytoplasmic) + negative FH = FH-deficient tumor; specificity >95%; 2SC positivity is the most specific biomarker of FH deficiency; used together: FH-/2SC+ = diagnostic of FH-deficient tumor

### FH mutation patterns

**Germline HLRCC mutations:**
- Missense: ~50% of HLRCC germline pathogenic variants; R190H (exon 3) is the most common missense pathogenic variant; affects catalytic histidine → enzyme dead; other hotspot: E319K
- Truncating (frameshift, nonsense): ~40%; unstable protein
- Splice site: ~10%; aberrant mRNA
- Large genomic rearrangements: <5%; detected by MLPA
- Autosomal dominant inheritance: heterozygous carrier at birth; somatic second hit (LOH or somatic truncating mutation) in each individual leiomyoma or RCC → biallelic FH LOF required for tumorigenesis

**Somatic FH in sporadic tumors:**
Sporadic FH-deficient RCC: somatic biallelic FH inactivation without germline mutation; ~1% of all RCC; identical histology and biology to HLRCC-associated RCC; detected by tumor molecular profiling (NGS) + FH IHC + 2SC IHC

## Function

### FH LOF and fumarate oncometabolite accumulation

**Fumarate as oncometabolite:** [^tomlinson-2002-fh]
FH biallelic LOF → fumarate cannot be converted to malate → fumarate accumulates to millimolar concentrations (10-100× normal); fumarate structural analogy to α-ketoglutarate (2-oxoglutarate) → competitive inhibition at the α-KG binding site of all 2-oxoglutarate-dependent dioxygenases:

1. **TET2 inhibition**: TET2 uses α-KG + O2 to oxidize 5-methylcytosine → 5-hydroxymethylcytosine (active demethylation step); fumarate blocks TET2 → DNA fails to demethylate → CpG island hypermethylation accumulates → epigenetic silencing of tumor suppressor genes and immune genes (CIMP-like phenotype)

2. **Histone KDM inhibition**: KDM4A (H3K9me3 demethylase), KDM5C (H3K4me3 demethylase), KDM6A (H3K27me3 demethylase): all use α-KG + O2 → fumarate competitively inhibits → histone marks accumulate → chromatin compaction → gene silencing

3. **PHD1/2/3 inhibition**: PHD enzymes hydroxylate HIF-1α at Pro-402 and Pro-564 → VHL E3 ligase recognition → HIF-1α ubiquitination → proteasomal degradation; fumarate inhibits PHDs → HIF-1α not hydroxylated → VHL cannot bind → HIF-1α stabilized → HIF-1α nuclear (despite normoxia) → VEGF, SLC2A1/GLUT1, PDK1, LDHA, CA9 → pseudohypoxic/Warburg phenotype [^linehan-2013-fh-review]

**KEAP1 succination:**
Fumarate undergoes nucleophilic addition to cysteine residues (succination): fumarate + Cys-SH → S-(2-succinyl)cysteine (2SC); KEAP1 (E3 ligase adaptor for NRF2) contains reactive cysteines (C273, C288) that are succinated in FH-deficient tumors → succinated KEAP1 cannot bind NRF2 → NRF2 nuclear accumulation → antioxidant response element (ARE) genes → NQO1, GCLC, HMOX1 → antioxidant upregulation; FH-deficient tumors are NRF2-activated (similar to KEAP1 mutation); this protects from reactive oxygen species generated by fumarate accumulation

**Epigenetic reprogramming in FH-deficient tumors:**
Combined TET2 + KDM inhibition → genome-wide DNA and histone hypermethylation; analogous to IDH-mutant CIMP (G-CIMP) and SDHB-deficient PHEO/PGL (hypermethylator phenotype); FH-deficient RCC: GSTP1 methylated (diagnostic in some labs); immune gene promoters (STING, IFN response genes) methylated → cold tumor microenvironment → resistance to PD-1/PD-L1 blockade monotherapy

## Mechanism

### Therapeutic targeting of FH-deficient tumors

**Bevacizumab + erlotinib (NCI Phase 2):**
- Rationale: FH LOF → HIF-1α → VEGF (angiogenesis) + EGFR signaling → dual blockade
- NCI HRCC Phase 2 study (Srinivasan et al.; N=30 HLRCC-associated RCC): bevacizumab (15 mg/kg IV q3w) + erlotinib (150 mg/day PO); ORR ~65-70% (remarkably high for RCC); mPFS ~21 months; OS data compelling; mechanism: anti-VEGF (bevacizumab) + EGFR inhibition (erlotinib potentiates anti-VEGF via reduced VEGF secretion through EGF-pERK-VEGF axis)
- This is the current standard of care for HLRCC-associated RCC (per NCI/NCCN guidelines)
- Erlotinib for EGFR: FH-deficient RCC is EGFR-overexpressing; erlotinib directly inhibits EGFR signaling; anti-EGFR synergizes with bevacizumab

**HIF-2α inhibitors:**
- Belzutifan (MK-6482): potent HIF-2α inhibitor; FDA-approved for VHL-associated ccRCC; being explored in FH-deficient RCC (both HIF-1α and HIF-2α active); Phase 2 study ongoing (NCT04895748); rationale: FH LOF activates both HIF isoforms; belzutifan may suppress HIF-2α targets (EPO, VEGF)
- MK-6482 mechanism: binds HIF-2α PAS-B domain → disrupts HIF-2α-ARNT (HIF-1β) heterodimerization → prevents HRE transcription → reduced VEGF, EPO, cyclin D1

**Checkpoint inhibitors in FH-deficient RCC:**
- HLRCC-associated RCC: immunologically cold (epigenetically silenced immune genes); PD-L1 expression low; TIL density low → ICB monotherapy response rates low (<10%)
- Combinations under investigation: pembrolizumab + lenvatinib (VEGFR+mTOR); ipilimumab + nivolumab; sunitinib; all being studied in FH-deficient RCC but no Phase 3 data yet
- Future: STING agonists + ICB may overcome epigenetic silencing of innate immunity in FH-deficient tumors

**FH in DNA repair context:**
Cytosolic FH accumulates at DSBs → fumarate production at DSB → local KDM2A inhibition → H3K36me2 at DSB → favors NHEJ; in FH-deficient cells, cytosolic FH absent → HR/NHEJ balance disrupted → elevated HR deficiency signature in some FH-deficient tumors → synthetic lethality with PARP inhibitors (Sulkowski 2018, Nat Genet); PARP inhibitor trials in FH-deficient tumors ongoing (olaparib + temozolomide)
