Networks of organs that share a common purpose.

## What this scale captures

A system is the scale at which integrated function emerges. The cardiovascular system pumps and distributes blood — a job no single organ can do. The immune system defends — but is composed of cells distributed across many organs (bone marrow, thymus, spleen, lymph nodes, mucosa). The nervous system computes — across ~86 billion neurons in the brain, the spinal cord, and the periphery.

Entries at this scale describe **the organs involved, how they coordinate, what regulatory loops exist between them, and what pathological states arise when the system fails as a whole** (rather than any one organ).

## Cross-atlas connections

- **Pathogens** that target a system rather than a single organ (sepsis affects the cardiovascular system; HIV affects the immune system) link in here.
- **Medicines** that act broadly on a system (vasodilators, immunosuppressants, anticonvulsants) often have their primary cross-link at this scale.
