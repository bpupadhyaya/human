# Atlas 01-Human · Scale 02: Atomic

This scale of the Human Atlas captures the elements and their ionic forms that make biology physically possible. At the atomic scale, the periodic table constrains which elements life can use — determined by their electron configurations, atomic radii, ionization energies, and electronegativity.

## What the atomic scale captures

| Category | Examples | Biological relevance |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Bulk elements (CHNOPS)** | Carbon, Hydrogen, Nitrogen, Oxygen, Phosphorus, Sulfur | The building blocks of all organic molecules — proteins, nucleic acids, lipids, carbohydrates |
| **Alkali metals** | Na⁺, K⁺ | Major extracellular (Na⁺) and intracellular (K⁺) cations; maintain membrane potential; osmolality |
| **Alkaline earth metals** | Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺ | Ca²⁺: universal second messenger, muscle contraction, neurotransmission, bone mineral. Mg²⁺: ATP cofactor, enzyme activator, bone |
| **Transition metals** | Fe²⁺/³⁺, Zn²⁺, Cu²⁺, Mn²⁺, Co²⁺ | Enzyme cofactors; redox-active in heme (hemoglobin, cytochromes); structural in zinc-finger proteins |
| **Halogens** | Cl⁻, I⁻ | Cl⁻: major anion balancing Na⁺; osmolarity and HCl in stomach. I⁻: essential for thyroid hormones (T3/T4) |
| **Trace elements** | Se, Mo, Cr, F | Selenocysteine in antioxidant enzymes; fluoride in dental mineral |

## Ions in biology

Most biologically relevant elements act as ions — atoms that have lost or gained electrons to achieve stable configurations. The ionic radius, charge density, and coordination chemistry of each ion determine which proteins will bind it and with what affinity.

- **Ca²⁺** (r = 0.099 nm, charge 2+) — perfect fit for EF-hand motifs in calmodulin, troponin C, and related proteins. Tight, selective binding enables Ca²⁺ to be a specific second messenger.
- **Na⁺** (r = 0.102 nm, charge 1+) — too low charge density for EF-hand binding; instead forms the resting membrane potential gradient through Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase.
- **K⁺** (r = 0.138 nm, charge 1+) — large; dominant intracellular cation; sets the resting potential; passes selectively through K⁺ channels whose selectivity filter exploits K⁺'s exact ionic radius.
- **Mg²⁺** (r = 0.072 nm, charge 2+) — stabilizes ATP (binds phosphates); activates hundreds of ATPases and kinases; different radius from Ca²⁺ enables discrimination despite same charge.
