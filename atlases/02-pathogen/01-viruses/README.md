# Atlas 02-Pathogen · Scale 01: Viruses

This scale of the Pathogen Atlas catalogs individual viruses and viral families that interact with human biology. Entries document viral structure, replication strategy, mechanism of host cell injury, immune signature, and cross-links to the relevant Human Atlas entries (cells, tissues, organs, and systems that are targeted).

## Scope

Viruses included here are selected for their documented interaction with human biology at one or more scales of the Human Atlas (01-human). Priority is given to viruses with:

- High global disease burden
- Significant cardiovascular or immunological impact
- Well-characterized molecular mechanisms suitable for multi-scale modeling
