# Class 06 — Microbiome

The commensal and mutualistic microorganisms that colonise the human body — primarily the gut, skin, oral cavity, and respiratory tract. The human gut microbiome contains ~38 trillion bacteria (roughly equal to human cell count), representing ~1,000 species. The microbiome modulates immunity, metabolism, neurotransmitter production, drug metabolism, and pathogen resistance. Dysbiosis (altered composition) is associated with IBD, obesity, type 2 diabetes, neurological disorders, and increased susceptibility to opportunistic pathogens.
