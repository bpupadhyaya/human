# Class 03 — Fungi

Eukaryotic pathogens. Unlike bacteria, fungi share the eukaryotic cell architecture of their human hosts — limiting drug targets to ergosterol synthesis (azoles, polyenes), β-glucan synthesis (echinocandins), and nucleic acid metabolism (flucytosine). Candida, Aspergillus, and Cryptococcus account for the majority of invasive fungal disease globally, predominantly in immunocompromised hosts.
