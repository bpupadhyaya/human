# Stream 02 — Traditional & Cultural Medicine

Healing knowledge developed and refined over millennia across every human culture. Entries in this stream catalogue traditional plant medicines, mineral preparations, and therapeutic practices — held to the same evidential standard as modern pharmaceuticals: what is the proposed mechanism, what clinical evidence exists, and where does the evidence fall short? Modern science gets no automatic priority; tradition gets no automatic pass.

Traditions covered include Ayurveda (Indian), Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), Greco-Arabic (Unani), African traditional medicine, Indigenous American medicine, and others.
