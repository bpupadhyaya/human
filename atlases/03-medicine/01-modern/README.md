# Atlas 03-Medicine · Scale 01: Modern Medicine

This scale of the Medicine Atlas catalogs modern pharmaceutical and biological treatments — drugs, biologics, vaccines, and device therapies developed through the scientific method, subjected to controlled clinical trials, and regulated by agencies such as the FDA, EMA, and WHO.

## Scope

Entries are organized by therapeutic category. Each entry documents:

- **Mechanism of action** at the molecular level, cross-linked to the relevant Human Atlas entry (the receptor, enzyme, or pathway targeted)
- **Clinical indications** and evidence base (landmark trials with citations)
- **Key agents** within the class
- **Pharmacology** (pharmacokinetics, selectivity, dosing principles)
- **Cross-links** to Human Atlas entries (what is targeted, what system is treated)
