# Atlas 03-Medicine · 01-Modern · 04-Cardio: Cardiovascular Medicines

This sub-scale catalogs modern cardiovascular pharmacology — drugs targeting the heart, vasculature, blood pressure, lipids, and coagulation system.

## Current entries

| Entry | Summary |
|:---|:---|
| [Beta-blockers](beta-blockers/README.md) | Competitive β1-adrenergic antagonists. First-line therapy for HFrEF, hypertension, angina, post-MI, and certain arrhythmias. |
| [Statins](statins/README.md) | HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors. Cornerstone of primary and secondary cardiovascular prevention. |

## Planned entries

- ACE inhibitors and ARBs — RAAS blockade for hypertension and heart failure
- Diuretics — loop (furosemide), thiazide, potassium-sparing / MRA (spironolactone, eplerenone)
- SGLT2 inhibitors — sodium-glucose cotransporter-2 inhibitors; emerging role in HFrEF and HFpEF
- Antiarrhythmics — class I–IV; amiodarone, sotalol, flecainide
- Anticoagulants — heparin, warfarin, DOACs (apixaban, rivaroxaban, dabigatran)
- Digoxin — cardiac glycoside; historical and current niche role
- Nitrates — NO donors for angina and acute decompensated heart failure
- Calcium channel blockers — dihydropyridines vs. non-dihydropyridines
