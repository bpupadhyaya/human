Multiple Sclerosis

Autoimmune demyelinating CNS disease mediated by autoreactive Th1/Th17 and CD8+ T cells attacking myelin; relapsing-remitting in 85% at onset. Natalizumab, ocrelizumab, and cladribine are high-efficacy DMTs; early aggressive therapy improves long-term disability outcomes.

Also known as: MS, multiple sclerosis, RRMS, PPMS, clinically isolated syndrome, CIS
