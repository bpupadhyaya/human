Rheumatoid Arthritis

Autoimmune synovitis from Th17/macrophage TNF-alpha and IL-6 activation; ACPA/anti-CCP antibodies are diagnostic. Methotrexate is first-line; TNF inhibitors (adalimumab), IL-6 blockade (tocilizumab), and JAK inhibitors (baricitinib) for refractory disease.

Also known as: RA, rheumatoid disease, adult RA, seropositive arthritis, inflammatory arthritis
