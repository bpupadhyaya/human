PIK3CA

Catalytic subunit of PI3K; phosphorylates PIP2 to PIP3, activating AKT-mTOR. Gain-of-function mutations (H1047R, E545K) in 25-40% of breast and 15% of colorectal cancers. Alpelisib (PI3K-alpha inhibitor) approved for PIK3CA-mutant HR+/HER2- breast cancer.

Also known as: PI3K, PI3Kalpha, PI3K p110alpha, phosphoinositide 3-kinase catalytic alpha, p110alpha
