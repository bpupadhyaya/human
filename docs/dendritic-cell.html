Dendritic Cell

Professional antigen-presenting cell bridging innate and adaptive immunity. Immature DCs patrol tissues; mature DCs upregulate MHC-II and migrate to lymph nodes to prime T cells. Two major subsets: cDC1/cDC2 and pDC.

Also known as: DC, professional APC, plasmacytoid DC, conventional DC
