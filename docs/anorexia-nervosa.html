Anorexia Nervosa

Anorexia nervosa (0.9% female lifetime; highest mortality of any psychiatric disorder ~5-10%/decade) involves reward circuit dysregulation, leptin deficiency, cortisol excess, and 5-HT dysregulation; only evidence-based treatment: nutritional rehabilitation + FBT or CBT-E.

Also known as: anorexia nervosa, AN, anorexia, restrictive AN, purging AN, EDE-Q, EDE, FBT, CBT-E, ARFID, refeeding syndrome, amenorrhea, weight restoration
