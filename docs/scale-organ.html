Level 06

Organ failure is the leading cause of ICU mortality

An organ is a discrete structure of multiple tissue types working together to perform a defined physiological function. At this scale, clinical medicine becomes directly legible — most diagnoses are organ-level events. These 14 entries cover the cardiovascular, neural, metabolic, digestive, renal, respiratory, and immune organ groups.

Cardiovascular
Heart
4-chamber pump  ·  ~5 L/min CO at rest  ·  Cardiomyocytes + conduction system  ·  Coronary arteries
~100K beats/day · 2.5 billion over a lifetime
Neural & Endocrine
Brain
~86B neurons  ·  100T synapses  ·  Cortex/limbic/brainstem/cerebellum  ·  Blood-brain barrier
~1.4 kg · 20% of body energy at rest
Hippocampus
Limbic memory organ  ·  CA1/CA3/DG  ·  LTP  ·  Adult neurogenesis  ·  Alzheimer's target
~40M neurons · first structure lost in Alzheimer's
Metabolic & Digestive
Liver
500+ metabolic functions  ·  Gluconeogenesis/glycogen storage  ·  Bile synthesis  ·  Drug metabolism
~1.5 kg · only organ with full regenerative capacity
Pancreas
Exocrine (digestive enzymes) + endocrine (insulin/glucagon)  ·  Islets of Langerhans
~98% exocrine · ~2% endocrine by mass
Stomach
HCl/pepsin secretion  ·  Parietal cells H⁺/K⁺-ATPase  ·  Intrinsic factor  ·  Gastrin
pH 1.5–3.5 lumen · ~1.5 L capacity
Small Intestine
Absorption surface ~250 m²  ·  SGLT1/GLUT2/peptide transporters  ·  Enteroendocrine axis
~6 m length · 90% nutrient absorption here
Large Intestine
Water/electrolyte absorption  ·  Microbiome colonisation  ·  SCFA production  ·  Mucus barrier
~1.5 m · ~38T microbial cells in colon
Adrenal Gland
Cortex (mineralocorticoids/glucocorticoids/androgens) + medulla (catecholamines)  ·  Stress axis
~8 g each · HPA axis terminus
Thyroid
T3/T4 synthesis via NIS/TPO  ·  Iodine economy  ·  TSH regulation  ·  Graves/Hashimoto
~20–30 g · sets metabolic rate for whole body
Renal
Kidney
~1M nephrons  ·  GFR 120 mL/min  ·  Filtration/reabsorption/secretion  ·  RAAS interface
~180 L plasma filtered/day · 1.5 L urine produced
Respiratory
Lung
~300M alveoli  ·  Total surface ~70 m²  ·  Gas exchange  ·  Surfactant  ·  Pulmonary circulation
~500 mL tidal volume · 12–20 breaths/min at rest
Immune
Spleen
Blood filtration  ·  Red pulp (senescent RBC removal)  ·  White pulp (lymphocyte niches)
~150 g · 250 mL blood reservoir capacity
Thymus
T cell maturation (positive/negative selection)  ·  MHC restriction  ·  Involutes with age
Peak mass ~30 g at puberty · replaced by fat in adulthood
