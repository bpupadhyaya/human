Atlas Three · The Medicine Atlas

An encyclopedia of every medicine humanity has used to heal.

Modern pharmaceuticals, traditional medical systems thousands of years old, and the foods and spices we already cook with — catalogued, cross-referenced, and free for every person, clinician, researcher, and AI agent on Earth.

Held to one standard: what is it, how does it work, what is the evidence, what does it interact with. Modern science gets no automatic priority; tradition gets no automatic pass. Both are studied — both belong to humanity.

Cancer Therapeutics — entries

CAR-T Cell Therapy
Chimeric antigen receptor T cells  ·  CD19 / BCMA  ·  Tisagenlecleucel, axicabtagene

Targeted Therapy
TKIs, mAbs, ADCs, PARP inhibitors  ·  Imatinib, osimertinib, trastuzumab, olaparib
