Opioid Use Disorder

Opioid use disorder (~2.7M US; ~80K deaths/year fentanyl) involves μ-opioid-mediated VTA disinhibition → NAcc dopamine surge, tolerance, and LC rebound withdrawal; MOUD: buprenorphine, methadone, naltrexone; naloxone reverses overdose.
