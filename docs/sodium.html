Biological Roles

Nav channels, Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase, NCX1, and secondary active co-transport

Voltage-Gated Sodium Channels — Action Potential

  Nav channel structure: 4 homologous domains (I–IV), each 6 TM helices
  Selectivity filter: DEKA (Asp-Glu-Lys-Ala) in P-loops → Na⁺ selective
  Gating: m³h model (Hodgkin-Huxley 1952)
    Resting: m gates closed, h gate open (−70 mV)
    Activation: depolarisation → m gates open rapidly → Na⁺ influx
    Inactivation: h gate closes within ~1 ms → inactivated state
    Recovery: membrane repolarisation → h gate reopens (refractory period)

  Nav1.5 (cardiac — SCN5A): Phase 0 depolarisation
    (dV/dt)max 200–300 V/s; drives ventricular activation wavefront
    Peak INa achieved at −30 mV; inactivated by −20 mV

  Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase (Jens Skou, Nobel 1997):
    3 Na⁺ out : 2 K⁺ in : 1 ATP hydrolysed per cycle
    Electrogenic → contributes ~−5 mV to resting Vm
    P-type ATPase: E1 (Na⁺ binds cyto) → E2 (Na⁺ released extra) →
    E2 (K⁺ binds extra) → E1 (K⁺ released cyto)
    α1: ubiquitous; α2: heart/brain; α3: neurons

  NCX1 (Na⁺/Ca²⁺ exchanger):
    3 Na⁺ : 1 Ca²⁺ (stoichiometry sets Vm-dependence)
    Forward mode (Vm < ENa_Ca equilibrium ~−50 mV): 3Na⁺ in → Ca²⁺ out (Ca²⁺ efflux = relaxation)
    Reverse mode (Vm > −50 mV, ischaemia): 3Na⁺ out → Ca²⁺ in (Ca²⁺ overload in ischaemia)

Absorption & Metabolism

ENaC/aldosterone axis; RAAS; ADH/aquaporin osmolality control

Na⁺ is freely filtered (25,000 mEq/day) and ~99% reabsorbed. PCT reabsorbs ~67% (NHE3 + Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase basolateral). TAL reabsorbs ~20% (NKCC2). DCT ~5% (NCC). CCD ~2% (ENaC — regulated by aldosterone). Aldosterone (via MR): ↑ SGK1 → ↑ ENaC surface expression; ↑ Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase. ADH (AVP): ↑ AQP2 insertion in CCD → water follows Na⁺ (fine-tuning osmolality).

Channelopathies

LQT3 (SCN5A gain-of-function): Nav1.5 incomplete inactivation → persistent late INa → APD prolongation → TdP. Brugada syndrome (SCN5A loss-of-function): ↓ INa in right ventricle → coved ST elevation V1-V3 → VF risk. Both treated with quinidine (INa/IKr blocker) or ICD implantation.
