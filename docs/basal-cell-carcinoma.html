Basal Cell Carcinoma

Most common human malignancy (~4 million/year in US); arises from basal keratinocytes driven by UV damage and PTCH1 loss. SMO mutations in ~40% of sporadic BCC activate Hedgehog-GLI → cyclin D1 and MYC; vismodegib and sonidegib are approved SMO inhibitors for advanced BCC.

Also known as: BCC, basal cell carcinoma, basal cell nevus syndrome, Gorlin syndrome, rodent ulcer, basal cell cancer, BCNevus
