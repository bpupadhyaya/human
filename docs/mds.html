Myelodysplastic Syndromes

Myelodysplastic syndromes are clonal hematopoietic disorders with cytopenias and dysplasia; SF3B1, DNMT3A, TET2, and TP53 mutations define subtypes. Azacitidine+venetoclax is higher-risk standard; luspatercept approved for SF3B1-mutant MDS; allo-SCT is the only cure.

Also known as: MDS, myelodysplastic syndrome, myelodysplasia, MDS-EB, refractory anemia, IPSS-R, azacitidine MDS, hypomethylating agent MDS, SF3B1 MDS, MDS-MPN
