Human Immunodeficiency Virus type 1

Lentivirus (Retroviridae). ~9.7 kb diploid ssRNA genome. CD4-tropic; gp120/gp41-mediated entry; reverse transcriptase drives rapid diversification; proviral integration establishes latent reservoir; causes AIDS via CD4+ T cell depletion.

Also known as: HIV, HIV type 1, human immunodeficiency virus, AIDS virus
