Adalimumab (Humira)

Fully human anti-TNFα IgG1 monoclonal antibody — neutralizes soluble and membrane-bound TNFα, blocking TNFR1/TNFR2 signaling, NF-κB activation, and the downstream inflammatory cytokine cascade driving RA, Crohn's disease, and psoriasis.

The world's best-selling drug by revenue from 2012 to 2022. 13+ FDA indications. Mandatory TB screening before use. Biosimilars now available since 2023 US patent expiry — a pivotal access milestone.

Adalimumab

Class: Fully Human Anti-TNFα IgG1 mAb  ·  Route: Subcutaneous injection  |  Brand: Humira (AbbVie); biosimilars: Amjevita, Hyrimoz, Hadlima  |  Indications: RA, PsA, AS, Crohn's, UC, psoriasis, JIA, uveitis, hidradenitis suppurativa

Adalimumab is a recombinant fully human IgG1 monoclonal antibody (phage display-derived, no murine sequences) that binds both soluble and membrane-bound TNFα with high affinity (Kd ~100 pM), blocking interaction with TNFR1 (p55) and TNFR2 (p75). TNFα blockade reduces NF-κB and MAPK/JNK activation in macrophages, synoviocytes, and intestinal epithelium — suppressing IL-1β, IL-6, IL-8, MMP, ICAM-1 production. Reverse signaling via membrane-TNFα induces anti-inflammatory IL-10 and T cell apoptosis. SC bioavailability ~64%; t½ ~14 days; every-other-week dosing (RA, PsA). Concomitant methotrexate reduces anti-drug antibody formation and improves clinical response.

Mechanism — TNFα Neutralization to NF-κB Suppression

  1. TNFα biology: TNFα is a 17 kDa homotrimeric cytokine produced primarily by macrophages, monocytes, and T cells; exists as soluble (sTNFα, cleaved by ADAM17/TACE) and membrane-bound (mTNFα) forms; signals through TNFR1 (constitutive, pro-inflammatory) and TNFR2 (inducible, immune cells)
  2. TNFR1 signaling cascade: TNFα-TNFR1 → TRADD → TRAF2 → RIP1 → IKK complex → IκB phosphorylation/ubiquitination/proteasomal degradation → NF-κB (RelA/p50) nuclear translocation → IL-1β, IL-6, IL-8, VCAM-1, ICAM-1, MMP-1/3/13, COX-2 gene transcription; also TRADD → FADD → caspase-8 → apoptosis; TRAF2 → MEKK1 → JNK/p38 → AP-1
  3. Adalimumab binding: Binds sTNFα and mTNFα trimer with high affinity (Kd ~100–200 pM) → steric blockade of TNFR1/TNFR2 binding → no receptor activation → NF-κB and MAPK remain inactive
  4. Reverse signaling (mTNFα): Adalimumab cross-links mTNFα → "outside-in" signaling → ↑ IL-10, ↓ pro-inflammatory cytokines, T cell apoptosis — additional anti-inflammatory mechanism beyond simple neutralization
  5. ADCC/CDC (secondary): IgG1 Fc region mediates ADCC (via NK cells/FcγRIII) and CDC (complement) against mTNFα-expressing macrophages/T cells in the synovium or gut wall — potentially depleting inflammatory effector cells

Safety — Critical Warnings

  • Tuberculosis reactivation (black box warning): TNFα is essential for granuloma maintenance — adalimumab disrupts granuloma integrity, releasing latent TB. Risk ~3–25× increased vs general population. Screen ALL patients with TST and/or IGRA before starting; treat latent TB with isoniazid × 9 months (start at least 1 month before adalimumab); chest X-ray in all patients
  • Serious bacterial, fungal, opportunistic infections: Elevated risk of bacterial infections; endemic mycoses (histoplasmosis, coccidioidomycosis, aspergillosis) — especially in relevant geographic areas; contraindicated in active serious infections; pause during serious infections
  • Hepatitis B reactivation: Screen HBsAg and anti-HBc before starting; HBsAg+ patients need antiviral prophylaxis (tenofovir or entecavir) throughout treatment and for 6 months after stopping
  • Lymphoma risk (2–3× increased relative risk): Risk concentrated in patients with longstanding active RA or IBD (underlying disease itself increases lymphoma risk); non-Hodgkin's lymphoma and hepatosplenic T-cell lymphoma (in young males with IBD + thiopurine combination) — thiopurine combination most concerning
  • Demyelinating disease: Contraindicated in pre-existing MS or optic neuritis; avoid in any demyelinating condition; new-onset demyelination reported (rare)
  • Heart failure exacerbation: Contraindicated in NYHA class III/IV; may worsen HF — TNFα has cardioprotective roles in cardiac remodeling
  • Anti-drug antibodies (ADA) / immunogenicity: ~10–15% of patients develop neutralizing anti-adalimumab antibodies → reduced drug levels → loss of efficacy; concomitant MTX substantially reduces ADA formation (~5% with MTX vs 15–20% without)
