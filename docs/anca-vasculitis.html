ANCA Vasculitis

ANCA vasculitis (GPA, MPA, EGPA): small-vessel necrotizing vasculitis; anti-PR3 or anti-MPO IgG primes neutrophils via C5a/C5aR1 → NET formation → endothelial injury. Cyclophosphamide/rituximab induction; avacopan (steroid-sparing; FDA Oct 2021) and rituximab maintenance.

Also known as: ANCA vasculitis, AAV, GPA, MPA, EGPA, granulomatosis with polyangiitis, microscopic polyangiitis, eosinophilic granulomatosis, Wegener's granulomatosis, Churg-Strauss syndrome, pauci-immune vasculitis, anti-PR3 vasculitis, anti-MPO vasculitis
