Renal System

Two kidneys, two ureters, bladder, and urethra. Filters 180 L plasma/day, maintains fluid/electrolyte/acid-base homeostasis, excretes nitrogenous waste, and produces EPO, renin, and calcitriol. Tightly coupled to cardiovascular system via RAAS.

Also known as: urinary system, renal-urinary system, excretory system
