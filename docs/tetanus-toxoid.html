Tetanus Toxoid (TT / Td / DTaP)

Formaldehyde-inactivated tetanospasmin from *Clostridium tetani*; >95% seroconversion after 3-dose primary series. Protective IgG ≥0.1 IU/mL. Given as TT, Td, DTaP, or DTP. Core WHO EPI vaccine; eliminated neonatal tetanus in >90% of countries. Boosters every 10 years.

Also known as: TT, Td, DTaP, DTP, tetanus toxoid, tetanus vaccine, diphtheria-tetanus-acellular pertussis
