IDH-Mutant Glioma

IDH-mutant glioma (Grade 2-3) defined by IDH1/2 mutations; 2-HG → TET2 inhibition → G-CIMP hypermethylation; WHO 2021 separates astrocytoma (ATRX LOF) from oligodendroglioma (1p/19q codeletion); vorasidenib FDA-approved August 2024 (INDIGO trial); median OS ~10-15 years.

Also known as: IDH-mutant glioma, IDH glioma, IDH mutant astrocytoma, IDH mutant oligodendroglioma, lower grade glioma, LGG, diffuse glioma IDH, IDH1 glioma, IDH1 R132H glioma, Grade 2 glioma vorasidenib
