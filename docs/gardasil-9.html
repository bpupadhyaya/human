Gardasil 9 (HPV9)

Nonavalent HPV L1 VLP vaccine; types 6/11/16/18/31/33/45/52/58 from *S. cerevisiae*. Prevents ~90% of cervical cancers and most HPV anogenital/oropharyngeal cancers. 2-dose (ages ≤14) or 3-dose schedule. FDA 2014; Swedish registry: 88% reduction in invasive cervical cancer.

Also known as: Gardasil-9, HPV9, 9-valent HPV vaccine, nonavalent HPV vaccine, V503
