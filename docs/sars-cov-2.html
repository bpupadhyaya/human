SARS-CoV-2

SARS-CoV-2 (betacoronavirus; sarbecovirus; ~29.9 kb +ssRNA) causes COVID-19 via ACE2/Spike entry; NSP1-16 replicase includes NSP12 RdRp (remdesivir target) and Mpro NSP5 (nirmatrelvir target); multiple IFN evasion proteins; Omicron R₀ >10; mRNA vaccines >90% vs severe disease.

Also known as: SARS-CoV-2, severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2, 2019-nCoV, COVID-19 virus, betacoronavirus, sarbecovirus, coronavirus
