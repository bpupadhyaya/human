SARS-CoV-2

Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome Coronavirus 2 — causative agent of COVID-19

~29.9 kb +ssRNA 100–160 nm virion 29 proteins ACE2 receptor >770 M confirmed cases >7 M deaths (official)

Infection Mechanism

  • Step 1 — Receptor binding & proteolytic priming Spike RBD binds ACE2 (angiotensin-converting enzyme 2) with ~10–15 nM affinity. Host serine protease TMPRSS2 cleaves S at S1/S2 and S2′ sites on the cell surface, exposing the fusion peptide. In ACE2-low cells, cathepsin B/L can substitute in the endosomal route. The furin site (PRRA) allows pre-activation in producer cells, broadening tropism to airway epithelia, alveolar type II cells, cardiomyocytes, endothelium, intestinal enterocytes, and renal tubular cells.
  • Step 2 — Membrane fusion, uncoating & genome delivery S2 heptad repeats HR1/HR2 form a 6-helix bundle driving fusion. The 29.9 kb RNA is deposited directly into the cytoplasm and is immediately translated by host ribosomes. Ribosomal frameshifting at the ORF1a/ORF1ab junction generates pp1a (~440 kDa) and pp1ab (~800 kDa), cleaved by nsp3 (PLpro) and nsp5 (3CLpro / Mpro) into 16 nsps.
  • Step 3 — Replicase complex assembly & transcription nsp12 (RdRp), nsp7, and nsp8 form the core polymerase; nsp13 (helicase), nsp14 (3′–5′ exonuclease proofreader + N7-methyltransferase), and nsp16 (2′-O-methyltransferase) complete the replisome. Nsp14/nsp16 methylation of the 5′ cap mimics host mRNA, enabling immune evasion. Nested subgenomic mRNAs (sgmRNAs) encode structural and accessory ORFs (3a, 6, 7a, 7b, 8, 9b, 10).
  • Step 4 — Innate immune evasion nsp1 binds the 40S ribosome, blocking host mRNA translation including ISG expression. nsp3 PLpro deubiquitinates IRF3 and STING, suppressing IFN-β induction. ORF3b (truncated) and ORF6 block STAT1/STAT2 nuclear translocation. nsp14 cap-methylation prevents MDA5/RIG-I recognition. Collectively, SARS-CoV-2 delays IFN-I induction by 2–4 days while viral loads peak — a key pathogenic window.
  • Step 5 — Cytokine storm & multi-organ damage In severe COVID-19, delayed IFN response is followed by dysregulated pro-inflammatory signalling: IL-6, IL-1β, TNF-α, IP-10/CXCL10 surge (the "cytokine storm"). In the lung, this drives diffuse alveolar damage (DAD), hyaline membrane formation, and ARDS. Endothelial ACE2 expression permits direct vascular infection causing endotheliitis, microvascular thrombosis, and complement activation. Myocardial injury occurs via direct cardiomyocyte infection (ACE2-high), hypoxaemia, and cytokine-driven inflammation (lymphocytic myocarditis). AKI results from tubular ACE2-mediated entry, haemodynamic compromise, and complement-mediated injury.

Treatment & Prevention

Nirmatrelvir/ritonavir (Paxlovid) Mpro (3CLpro) inhibitor; ritonavir pharmacokinetic booster. For high-risk non-hospitalised adults within 5 days of symptom onset. Reduces hospitalisation/death by ~89% (EPIC-HR trial). Watch drug–drug interactions (CYP3A4). Rebound phenomenon reported in ~5%.
Remdesivir (Veklury) Adenosine analogue; inhibits nsp12 RdRp. IV infusion; EUA/full approval for hospitalised patients. Modest benefit in early disease; limited impact in severe ARDS.
Molnupiravir (Lagevrio) Ribonucleoside analogue → mutagenesis of viral RNA. ~30% reduction in hospitalisation (MOVe-OUT). Second-line when Paxlovid not feasible; teratogenicity concerns limit use in pregnancy.
Dexamethasone (RECOVERY trial) 6 mg/day × 10 days for patients requiring supplemental oxygen/ventilation. Reduces 28-day mortality by 35% in ventilated patients (RECOVERY, Horby et al. 2021). Cornerstone of hospitalised COVID management. NOT beneficial in mild disease without hypoxaemia.
IL-6 receptor antagonists Tocilizumab, sarilumab — for severe/critical COVID with elevated CRP and requiring supplemental O₂. Reduce mortality and ICU stay (RECOVERY, REMAP-CAP trials).
JAK inhibitors Baricitinib — immunomodulatory; ACTT-2/RECOVERY trial support; reduces mortality in hospitalised patients on oxygen.
Anticoagulation Prophylactic LMWH for all hospitalised patients; therapeutic anticoagulation for documented VTE or severe coagulopathy.
mRNA vaccines (BNT162b2 / mRNA-1273) LNP-encapsulated mRNA encoding prefusion-stabilised spike. >90% efficacy against symptomatic disease (original strain); bivalent and updated XBB.1.5/JN.1-targeting formulations maintain effectiveness against severe disease from newer variants. 2-dose primary + booster schedule; annual update recommended.
Adenoviral vector vaccines ChAdOx1 nCoV-19 (AstraZeneca/Vaxzevria), Ad26.COV2.S (Janssen) — widely deployed globally; rare VITT risk with ChAdOx1 led to restriction in younger age groups in some countries.
Protein subunit & other platforms NVX-CoV2373 (Novavax, spike + Matrix-M adjuvant); inactivated virus vaccines (CoronaVac, Covaxin); widely used in lower-income settings.
