Pemphigus Vulgaris

Pemphigus vulgaris (PV) is an IgG4-mediated autoimmune blistering disease targeting Dsg3 (mucous membranes) and Dsg1 (skin); suprabasal acantholysis. Rituximab (PEMPHIX: 90% vs 28% CR; FDA Jun 2018) and efgartigimod (ADHERE-SC; FDA Oct 2023) are approved therapies.

Also known as: pemphigus vulgaris, PV, pemphigus, pemphigus foliaceus, PF, autoimmune blistering disease, AIBD, anti-Dsg3, intraepidermal pemphigus
