Social Anxiety Disorder

Social anxiety disorder (12% lifetime) involves amygdala hyperreactivity to social threat and striatal dopamine deficit impairing social reward; first-line: SSRIs/SNRIs + CBT with social exposure; β-blockers for performance-only type; MAOIs for severe refractory cases.

Also known as: social anxiety disorder, social phobia, SAD, performance anxiety, LSAS, Liebowitz Social Anxiety Scale, social fear, public speaking anxiety
