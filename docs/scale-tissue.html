Tissue architecture determines function

The four primary tissue types — epithelial, connective, muscle, and nervous — underpin every organ in the body. At the tissue scale, cellular specialization is visible as physical organization: layers, tubes, sheets, syncytia, and lattices that define how a structure performs its physiological role. These 14 entries span cardiac, vascular, bone, renal, respiratory, neural, hepatic, endocrine, and gastrointestinal tissues.

Cardiac & Vascular
Myocardium
Cardiac muscle tissue  ·  Cardiomyocyte syncytium  ·  Systolic/diastolic function  ·  Frank-Starling mechanism
Striated involuntary muscle · ~2–4 billion cardiomyocytes
Endocardium
Inner cardiac lining  ·  Paracrine hub (NO, ET-1)  ·  Valve origin  ·  Infective endocarditis
Endothelial monolayer · regulates cardiomyocyte contractility
Cardiac Conduction System
SA node → AV node → His-Purkinje  ·  AP propagation  ·  Arrhythmia substrate
60–100 bpm intrinsic pacemaker rate · HCN4 channels
Arterial Wall
Intima/media/adventitia  ·  Endothelium + SMC + ECM  ·  Atherosclerosis
3-layer architecture · tunica media governs compliance
Bone & Connective
Bone Marrow
Red (haematopoietic) vs yellow (adipose)  ·  HSC niche  ·  Haematopoiesis
~500 billion blood cells produced per day
Cortical Bone
Osteon/Haversian system  ·  Mineralisation  ·  Bone remodelling unit  ·  Hormonal signalling
~80% of skeletal mass · 10-year complete remodelling cycle
Renal
Glomerulus
Filtration barrier (podocyte/GBM/endothelium)  ·  Starling equation  ·  GFR 120 mL/min
~1M glomeruli per kidney · 180 L plasma filtered per day
Respiratory
Alveolus
Type I + Type II pneumocyte  ·  Surfactant layer  ·  Fick diffusion  ·  V̇/Q̇ matching
~300M alveoli · 70 m² total surface · 0.2–0.3 µm membrane
Neural
Synapse
SNARE vesicle fusion  ·  LTP/LTD  ·  Neurotransmitter reuptake
~100T synapses · ~1 ms transmission time
Hippocampus
CA1/CA3/DG trisynaptic circuit  ·  Adult neurogenesis  ·  Memory encoding
~40M neurons (human) · dentate gyrus generates new neurons
Hepatic
Hepatic Lobule
Portal triad → sinusoid → central vein  ·  Metabolic zonation  ·  Classic/portal/acinus lobule models
~50–100K lobules per liver · zone 1–3 metabolic gradient
Endocrine
Islet of Langerhans
α (glucagon), β (insulin), δ (somatostatin) cells  ·  GSIS circuit  ·  T1D autoimmune target
~1M islets per pancreas · ~1% of pancreatic mass
Gastrointestinal
Intestinal Epithelium
Villus/crypt  ·  Enterocyte/goblet/enteroendocrine/Paneth  ·  Tight junctions  ·  Microbiome interface
~250 m² surface area · complete turnover every 3–5 days
