Shingrix (RZV)

Adjuvanted recombinant subunit zoster vaccine; VZV glycoprotein E antigen + AS01B adjuvant (MPL + QS-21 liposomes). Two IM doses 2–6 months apart. ZOE-50: 97% efficacy in adults ≥50; ZOE-70: 91% in ≥70. Preferred over live-attenuated Zostavax; approved for immunocompromised.
