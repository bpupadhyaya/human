Epstein-Barr Virus

EBV (HHV-4; gammaherpesvirus; 172 kb dsDNA) infects >95% of adults; latency programs I/II/III expressing EBNA/LMP antigens drive oncogenesis; associated with Burkitt lymphoma, Hodgkin lymphoma, NPC, PTLD; infectious mononucleosis in adolescents; no approved vaccine or antiviral.
