Endometrial Cancer

Most common gynecologic malignancy in the US (~67,000/year); POLE-ultramutated and MSI-H subtypes respond to pembrolizumab; endometrioid type driven by PTEN/PIK3CA/KRAS; serous by TP53/ERBB2. Carboplatin+paclitaxel is standard; dostarlimab approved for dMMR endometrial cancer.

Also known as: endometrial cancer, endometrial carcinoma, uterine cancer, endometrioid adenocarcinoma, uterine serous carcinoma, POLE-ultramutated, Lynch-associated endometrial cancer
