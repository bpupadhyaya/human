Desmoid Tumor

Desmoid tumor (aggressive fibromatosis) is a locally invasive spindle cell neoplasm; ~80% harbor CTNNB1 activating mutations; no metastatic potential; nirogacestat (gamma-secretase inhibitor, DeFi trial) FDA-approved 2023; sorafenib active; watch-and-wait for stable disease.

Also known as: desmoid tumor, aggressive fibromatosis, desmoid fibromatosis, deep fibromatosis, desmoid CTNNB1, FAP desmoid, mesenteric fibromatosis, sporadic desmoid, beta-catenin fibromatosis, APC desmoid
