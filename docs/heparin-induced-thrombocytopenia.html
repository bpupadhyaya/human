Heparin-Induced Thrombocytopenia

Heparin-induced thrombocytopenia (HIT type 2) is an immune thrombocytopenia from anti-PF4/heparin IgG; platelet activation → paradoxical thrombosis. Stop heparin; switch to argatroban/bivalirudin/fondaparinux; avoid warfarin initially. 4T score guides clinical probability.

Also known as: HIT, heparin-induced thrombocytopenia, HIT type 2, HITT, heparin thrombocytopenia, anti-PF4 antibody, PF4-heparin antibody, VITT
