Dexamethasone

Potent synthetic glucocorticoid (25× cortisol) — GR-mediated genomic transactivation (ANXA1, DUSP1, IκBα) plus physical transrepression of NF-κB/AP-1, collectively suppressing the entire pro-inflammatory cytokine network.

Zero mineralocorticoid activity. The 2020 RECOVERY trial established it as the first treatment to reduce COVID-19 mortality. Also transforms outcomes in croup, cerebral edema, anaphylaxis, and chemotherapy-induced emesis.

Dexamethasone

Class: Synthetic Fluorinated Glucocorticoid  ·  Routes: PO, IV, IM, topical, intrathecal, intraocular  |  Brand: Decadron, Dexasone, Maxidex  |  Mineralocorticoid activity: ~0

Dexamethasone binds cytoplasmic GR (NR3C1) with ~10× greater affinity than cortisol, driving nuclear translocation and two complementary anti-inflammatory mechanisms: (1) GRE-dependent transactivation upregulating ANXA1 (inhibits PLA2 → ↓ arachidonic acid), DUSP1 (inactivates JNK/p38 → ↓ AP-1), and IκBα (sequesters NF-κB); (2) GR tethering to NF-κB RelA/p65 and AP-1 → direct transrepression without DNA binding. The net result is comprehensive suppression of IL-1β, TNFα, IL-6, IL-8, COX-2, iNOS, and VCAM-1. Its uniquely long biological t½ (36–72 h) and zero mineralocorticoid activity make it the preferred glucocorticoid for many acute and critical indications.

Mechanism — GR Transactivation + NF-κB Transrepression

  1. GR binding and nuclear translocation: Dexamethasone crosses the plasma membrane and binds cytoplasmic GR (NR3C1) with high affinity (Ki ~5 nM; ~10× vs cortisol). GR:dex complex dissociates from Hsp90/FKBP51 chaperone → conformational change → nuclear translocation via importin-α/β
  2. Transactivation — GRE-dependent: GR homodimerizes and binds palindromic GREs in target gene promoters → activates: ANXA1/lipocortin-1 (inhibits PLA2 → ↓ arachidonic acid → ↓ all prostanoids and leukotrienes), DUSP1/MKP-1 (inactivates JNK and p38 MAPK → ↓ AP-1), IκBα (directly sequesters NF-κB in cytoplasm), and IL-10 (anti-inflammatory cytokine)
  3. Transrepression — NF-κB tethering: GR monomer physically interacts with RelA (p65) subunit of NF-κB via protein-protein contact → mutual inhibition; GR does not bind DNA in this mode — pure protein-protein repression. Targets repressed: IL-1β, TNFα, IL-6, IL-8 (CXCL8), COX-2, iNOS, MMP-9, ICAM-1, VCAM-1
  4. Non-genomic effects (rapid): Membrane-associated GR and physicochemical effects on lipid bilayer → rapid inhibition of PLA2 within minutes; important for immediate clinical effects (airway edema, anaphylaxis)

The zero mineralocorticoid activity means dexamethasone does not cause sodium retention, hypertension, or hypokalemia — critical for high-dose use in cerebral edema, where fluid retention would worsen intracranial pressure.

RECOVERY Trial — COVID-19

Trial: RECOVERY (Randomised Evaluation of COVid-19 thERapY) — UK platform trial, 2020. Dexamethasone 6 mg OD × 10 days vs usual care in 6,425 hospitalized COVID-19 patients.

Key results (28-day mortality):

  • Ventilated patients: 29% mortality reduction (29.3% vs 41.4%; RR 0.64, 95% CI 0.51–0.81)
  • O₂ without ventilation: 20% mortality reduction (23.3% vs 26.2%; RR 0.82)
  • No O₂ required: No benefit — possibly slightly harmful (22.9% vs 18.8%)
  • NNT: ~8 for ventilated patients; ~25 for O₂-requiring patients
  • Mechanism in COVID-19: Severe COVID-19 is characterized by a dysregulated immune response ("cytokine storm") — IL-6, IL-1β, TNFα driven immunopathological lung injury; dexamethasone suppresses this destructive inflammation in the late-stage disease (days 7–14+) when viral replication is declining

Adverse Effects

  • Hyperglycemia: Dose-dependent; gluconeogenesis ↑ + insulin resistance → BG rises 5–10+ mmol/L; monitor and manage with insulin sliding scale in diabetics and non-diabetics on high-dose therapy
  • HPA axis suppression: Exogenous glucocorticoid suppresses ACTH → adrenal atrophy; duration and dose dependent; abrupt cessation after >2–3 weeks causes adrenal insufficiency — taper required for prolonged courses
  • Osteoporosis (long-term): Bone density loss 5–10% in first year via ↓ osteoblast function, ↑ osteoclast activity, ↓ intestinal calcium absorption; prescribe calcium + vitamin D; consider bisphosphonate for >3 months therapy
  • Cushing's syndrome (long-term): Moon face, truncal obesity, violaceous striae, proximal myopathy, easy bruising; dose and duration dependent
  • Neuropsychiatric: Insomnia (common), euphoria, mania, depression; severe psychiatric reactions rare but documented; morning dosing reduces insomnia
  • Immunosuppression: Increased susceptibility to bacterial, fungal, viral infections; impairs wound healing; consider PCP prophylaxis (co-trimoxazole) for >20 mg prednisolone-equivalent/day × >4 weeks
