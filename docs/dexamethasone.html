Dexamethasone

Potent synthetic glucocorticoid (25× cortisol potency); GR-mediated genomic suppression of NF-κB/AP-1 → ↓ IL-1/6/8/TNF. Zero mineralocorticoid activity. RECOVERY trial: 17% mortality ↓ in severe COVID-19. Also used for cerebral edema, anaphylaxis, adrenal crisis, antiemesis.

Also known as: dexamethasone, Decadron, Dexasone, Maxidex, dexamethasone sodium phosphate, 9α-fluoro-16α-methylprednisolone
