Medulloblastoma

Medulloblastoma is the most common pediatric brain tumor; 4 molecular subgroups: WNT (~10%, OS ~95%), SHH (~38%), Group 3 (~25%, MYC-amplified, worst prognosis), Group 4 (~35%); surgery + CSI + chemotherapy; SHH MB responsive to SMO inhibitors; infant MB: chemo without radiation.

Also known as: medulloblastoma, MB, pediatric medulloblastoma, SHH medulloblastoma, WNT medulloblastoma, Group 3 medulloblastoma, cerebellar medulloblastoma, MBEN
