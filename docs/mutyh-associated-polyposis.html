MUTYH-Associated Polyposis

MUTYH-associated polyposis (MAP) is an autosomal recessive CRC predisposition syndrome caused by biallelic MUTYH mutations; 10-100 colorectal adenomas; CRC risk ~40-75% lifetime; two founder variants (Y179C, G396D) in most Western patients; annual colonoscopy from age 25.

Also known as: MAP, MUTYH-associated polyposis, MYH-associated polyposis, biallelic MUTYH, MUTYH polyposis, MAP CRC, MYH polyposis, autosomal recessive polyposis, MAP colorectal
