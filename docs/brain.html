Brain

The ~1.4 kg central organ of the nervous system: site of cognition, perception, memory, emotion, and motor control. Contains ~86 billion neurons in 4 cortical lobes, subcortical nuclei, cerebellum, and brainstem. Protected by skull, meninges, and the blood-brain barrier.

Also known as: encephalon, cerebrum, cerebral cortex, human brain
