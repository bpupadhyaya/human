HIF-1α Oxygen Sensing

PHD1/2/3 (prolyl hydroxylases) hydroxylate HIF-1α Pro402/564 in normoxia → VHL/E3 ubiquitylation → proteasomal degradation. Hypoxia: PHDs inactivated → HIF-1α stabilises → VEGF/EPO/GLUT1/LDH-A upregulation. HIF-2α (EPAS1) drives EPO in renal fibroblasts. PHD inhibitors (roxadustat, vadadustat) stabilise HIF for CKD anaemia treatment.

Absorption & Metabolism

Respiratory physiology, haemoglobin binding kinetics, tissue extraction

O₂ crosses the alveolar-capillary membrane by simple diffusion (Fick's law; distance ~0.3 µm; area ~70 m²). Dissolved O₂ is minor (0.003 mL/dL/mmHg); ~98.5% is transported as oxyhaemoglobin (HbO₂). The oxyhaemoglobin dissociation curve is sigmoidal (Hill coefficient ~2.8) due to cooperativity in the α₂β₂ tetramer.
