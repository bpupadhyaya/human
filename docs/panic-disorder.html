Panic Disorder

Panic disorder (2-3% lifetime) involves recurrent unexpected panic attacks driven by LC-NE hyperactivation and amygdala CO2 hypersensitivity; first-line: SSRIs/SNRIs + CBT with interoceptive exposure; benzodiazepines for acute attacks; avoid long-term BZ use.

Also known as: panic disorder, panic attack, agoraphobia, CO2 hypersensitivity, Klein suffocation alarm, interoceptive exposure, unexpected panic, anticipatory anxiety
