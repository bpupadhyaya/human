Human Engineering

A unified model of human biology — from subatomic particles to the whole body.

So we can engineer immunity, vaccines, and medicines at the speed of mutation — not years behind it.

Mission

Model the human body as a single, navigable system across every scale of organization — from quarks and electrons up to organs, systems, and whole-body physiology. Make that model precise enough that we can engineer, reverse-engineer, and re-engineer human biology with the same rigor we apply to silicon and software.

The end goal: when the next fast-mutating pathogen appears, a candidate vaccine or therapy is hours away — not months or years. And eventually, an adaptive immune layer that generalizes to threats we haven't seen yet.

The Scale Ladder

Biology is the same system viewed at different magnifications. Each level constrains and explains the next. The model has to be coherent across all of them.

Level 01

Subatomic

Quarks, electrons, photons — the substrate of all chemistry.

Level 02

Atomic

Elements and bonds that make biology physically possible.

Level 03

Molecular

Proteins, nucleic acids, lipids — the working parts of cells.

Level 04

Cellular

The smallest unit that's unambiguously alive.

Level 05

Tissue

Cells specialized and organized into functional fabrics.

Level 06

Organ

Tissues coordinated into a single functional unit.

Level 07

System

Organs networked — immune, nervous, circulatory, endocrine.

Level 08

Whole Body

An integrated organism — the actual unit we care about.

Four Capabilities

If we have the model, four things become possible.

Engineer

Design new biology — proteins, cells, tissues, immune responses — from first principles, with predictable behavior.

Reverse-engineer

Take an existing pathogen, disease, or healthy mechanism and recover its mechanism end-to-end.

Re-engineer

Repair, optimize, or replace components of human biology with the same confidence as a hardware swap.

Immunize

Design adaptive defenses that generalize across fast-mutating threats — not chase each variant in retrospect.

Why now

COVID-19 made the cost of slowness legible: every month between a pathogen emerging and a vaccine shipping is paid in lives, economies, and trust. Next time, the gap should be measured in hours.

The compute, the data, the modeling tools, and the molecular biology have all crossed thresholds at roughly the same time. A unified, multi-scale model of the human body is no longer obviously science fiction — it's a hard, concrete engineering project.
