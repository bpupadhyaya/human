Chronic Kidney Disease

Progressive irreversible loss of kidney function (GFR <60 mL/min for >3 months) from diabetes, hypertension, or glomerulonephritis. Staged G1-G5 by GFR; complications include anemia, hyperparathyroidism, and uremia. End-stage managed by dialysis or transplantation.

Also known as: CKD, chronic renal failure, chronic renal insufficiency, end-stage kidney disease, ESKD
