Hypertension

Sustained BP ≥130/80 mmHg (AHA 2017); 1.28 billion adults globally. Primary (~95%): RAAS overactivation, sympathetic excess, endothelial NO deficiency. Complications: stroke, MI, HFpEF, CKD. First-line: ACE-I/ARBs, thiazides, CCBs.

Also known as: hypertension, high blood pressure, HTN, arterial hypertension, essential hypertension
