Atlas One › Scales › Level 04 — Cellular

The cell is the fundamental unit of biology — the smallest structure that grows, replicates, responds to environment, and dies by program or accident. The human body maintains 37.2 trillion of them simultaneously: immune cells patrolling for pathogens and tumors, cardiomyocytes firing 100,000 times a day for a lifetime, neurons holding synaptic weights that encode memory, hepatocytes performing thousands of metabolic reactions per second. Each cell type in the atlas is modeled with its origin, defining proteins, signaling state, functional role, failure modes, and connections to the molecules below and the tissues above it.

Immune, Cardiovascular, Neural, and Metabolic Cells

28 entries organized by functional class. Each entry links structure, signaling, tissue context, and disease relevance.

Immune Cells

Macrophage
Monocyte-derived  ·  M1/M2 polarization  ·  Tissue-resident (Kupffer, microglia, alveolar)
Phagocytosis; antigen presentation; cytokine release; foam cell in atherosclerosis; M2 in fibrosis
Natural Killer Cell
Innate lymphocyte  ·  CD56&sup+;CD3−  ·  KIR/NKG2D activating/inhibitory balance
MHC-I missing self surveillance; perforin/granzyme cytotoxicity; ADCC; NK cell deficiency; NK cell therapy
B Cell
Adaptive lymphocyte  ·  BCR surface Ig  ·  Germinal center / somatic hypermutation
Antibody production; antigen presentation to Tfh; class switching (IgM→IgG/A/E); CLL; rituximab (anti-CD20)
T Helper Cell (CD4&sup+;)
Adaptive  ·  Th1/Th2/Th17/Tfh/Treg subsets  ·  MHC-II restricted
B-cell help; macrophage activation; IFN-γ/IL-4/IL-17 cytokine programs; HIV CD4 depletion; checkpoint therapy
T Cytotoxic Cell (CD8&sup+;)
Adaptive  ·  MHC-I restricted  ·  TCR + co-stimulation
Perforin/granzyme killing; Fas-FasL apoptosis; viral clearance; tumor immunosurveillance; CAR-T engineering
Regulatory T Cell (Treg)
CD4&sup+;CD25&sup+;FOXP3&sup+;  ·  Thymus-derived & induced peripheral  ·  IL-2 dependent
IL-10/TGF-β immunosuppression; CTLA-4 constitutive expression; IPEX (FOXP3 mutation); Treg depletion in cancer therapy
Dendritic Cell
Myeloid (cDC1/cDC2) / Plasmacytoid (pDC)  ·  Professional APC  ·  MHC-I cross-presentation (cDC1)
T-cell priming; CCR7 lymph node homing; type I IFN (pDC); vaccine adjuvant target; tolerogenic DC in autoimmunity
Plasma Cell
Terminal B-cell differentiation  ·  Blimp-1/IRF4  ·  High secretory ER mass
1000–10,000 Ab/sec; long-lived bone marrow niche; multiple myeloma; BCMA-targeted therapy; UPR dependence
Mast Cell
Tissue-resident  ·  FcεRI  ·  IgE-sensitized degranulation
Histamine/tryptase/heparin granules; IgE crosslinking → anaphylaxis; anti-parasite defense; urticaria; omalizumab
Neutrophil
Polymorphonuclear granulocyte  ·  Most abundant circulating leukocyte  ·  Short-lived (~6–8 h)
Phagocytosis; ROS burst; NET formation; first responder to infection; neutropenia risk; G-CSF mobilisation
Platelet
Anucleate megakaryocyte fragment  ·  150–400 × 10&sup9;/L  ·  GPIb/IIb-IIIa surface receptors
Primary hemostasis; TXA2/ADP amplification; GPIIb-IIIa fibrinogen bridge; antiplatelet drugs (aspirin/clopidogrel); ITP

Cardiovascular Cells

Erythrocyte (Red Blood Cell)
Anucleate biconcave disc  ·  120-day lifespan  ·  5 million per µL
Hemoglobin O&sub2; transport; CO&sub2; as bicarbonate; Band 3 / spectrin cytoskeleton; sickle cell; thalassaemia; G6PD deficiency
Cardiomyocyte
Striated muscle  ·  Binucleate  ·  Intercalated discs with Cx43
~100,000 contractions/day; sarcomere Ca²&sup+;-troponin; ETC dependence; myocarditis; HCM/DCM mutations; limited regeneration
SA Node Cell
Pacemaker cardiomyocyte  ·  ~1% of cardiac cells  ·  HCN4 / L-type Ca²&sup+; dominant
I&sub⊂ (HCN4) spontaneous depolarization; sympathetic/vagal autonomic modulation; sick sinus; ivabradine; pacemaker therapy
Endothelial Cell
Inner lining of all vessels  ·  ~10¹¹ cells total  ·  eNOS / VWF / ICAM-1
Barrier function; eNOS NO; coagulation balance; leukocyte transmigration (ICAM-1/VCAM-1); endotheliopathy; atherosclerosis
Smooth Muscle Cell
Vascular media  ·  Contractile / synthetic phenotype  ·  α-SMA / SM22α
Vascular tone (Ca²&sup+;/IP&sub3;); phenotypic switching in atherosclerosis; vascular fibrosis; PDGF proliferation; calcium-channel blockers

Neural Cells

Neuron
Excitable  ·  ~86 billion (brain)  ·  Axon / dendrite / soma compartments
Action potential; synaptic transmission; LTP/LTD; neurodegenerative vulnerability; Nav1.x/Kv/Ca&sub²&sup+; channel pharmacology
Astrocyte
GFAP&sup+; glial cell  ·  ~50% of brain cells  ·  Tripartite synapse partner
Glutamate reuptake (GLT-1/GLAST); K&sup+; spatial buffering; BBB astrocyte end-feet; reactive astrogliosis; glymphatic clearance
Microglia
Brain-resident macrophage  ·  Yolk sac origin  ·  Iba1&sup+;/TMEM119&sup+;
Synaptic pruning; neuroinflammation (M1/M2-like); TREM2 Alzheimer risk; CSF1R inhibitors; homeostatic vs. disease-associated
Oligodendrocyte
Myelinating CNS glial  ·  One cell myelinates up to 50 axons  ·  MBP/PLP
Saltatory conduction (nodes of Ranvier); multiple sclerosis (demyelination); oligodendrocyte precursor (OPC) remyelination

Metabolic & Structural Cells

Hepatocyte
Parenchymal liver cell  ·  ~60% of liver cells  ·  Periportal-to-pericentral zonation
Albumin/coagulation factor synthesis; gluconeogenesis; cytochrome P450 drug metabolism; bile acid conjugation; NAFLD→NASH
Adipocyte
White / brown / beige  ·  Lipid-filled unilocular (WAT) · Multilocular (BAT)
Triglyceride storage/lipolysis; leptin/adiponectin endocrine; BAT thermogenesis (UCP1); visceral adiposity risk; GLP-1 targets
Fibroblast
Mesenchymal stromal cell  ·  Vimentin&sup+;/CD90&sup+;  ·  Context-dependent phenotypes
Collagen/ECM synthesis; myofibroblast differentiation in fibrosis; TGF-β signaling; wound healing; cardiac fibrosis
Podocyte
Highly specialized visceral epithelial  ·  Glomerular slit diaphragm  ·  Nephrin/Podocin/TRPC6
Filtration barrier (slit diaphragm); podocyte injury → nephrotic syndrome; FSGS; minimal change disease; limited regeneration
Osteoblast
Bone-forming mesenchymal  ·  Runx2/Osterix  ·  Osteocalcin/type I collagen secreted
Hydroxyapatite mineralization; Wnt/BMP signaling; PTH anabolic vs. catabolic; osteoporosis; teriparatide mechanism
Osteoclast
Multinucleated macrophage-derived  ·  RANKL/RANK/OPG axis  ·  Cathepsin K/H&sup+;-ATPase
Bone resorption via HCl + protease; RANKL inhibition (denosumab); bisphosphonate mechanism; Paget disease; metastatic bone disease
Type II Pneumocyte
Alveolar epithelial  ·  Lamellar body surfactant secretion  ·  Progenitor for Type I
Surfactant (DPPC/SP-A/B/C/D); alveolar surface tension; ARDS; neonatal RDS; SARS-CoV-2 ACE2 entry; ILD fibrosis progression
