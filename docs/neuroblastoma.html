Neuroblastoma

Neuroblastoma is the most common extracranial pediatric solid tumor; neural crest-derived; MYCN amplification in ~40% high-risk; tandem auto-SCT + dinutuximab (anti-GD2) + 13-cis-retinoic acid maintenance for high-risk; ALK inhibitors in Phase 3 trials.

Also known as: neuroblastoma, NB, high-risk neuroblastoma, neuroblastoma MYCN, Stage 4 neuroblastoma, N-myc neuroblastoma, INRG neuroblastoma
