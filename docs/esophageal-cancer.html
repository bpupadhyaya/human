Esophageal Cancer

Esophageal cancer includes ESCC (squamous, smoking/alcohol) and EAC (adenocarcinoma, Barrett's/HER2+ ~15%); nivolumab+chemotherapy (CheckMate 648) is first-line for ESCC; trastuzumab+chemotherapy (ToGA) and T-DXd for HER2+ EAC; 5-year OS ~20%.

Also known as: esophageal cancer, ESCC, esophageal squamous cell carcinoma, EAC, esophageal adenocarcinoma, Barrett's esophagus cancer, GEJ cancer, gastroesophageal junction cancer, CheckMate 648, ATTRACTION-3
