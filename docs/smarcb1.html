SMARCB1

SMARCB1 (INI1/BAF47) is a core SWI/SNF BAF complex subunit; biallelic LOF in ~100% AT/RT, rhabdoid tumors, and epithelioid sarcoma; SMARCB1 loss → PRC2/EZH2-mediated H3K27me3 at tumor suppressor loci; tazemetostat (EZH2 inhibitor) FDA-approved for epithelioid sarcoma.

Also known as: SMARCB1, INI1, BAF47, SNF5, hSNF5, SMARCB1 rhabdoid, INI1 loss, rhabdoid tumor predisposition, RTPS1, SWI/SNF tumor suppressor
