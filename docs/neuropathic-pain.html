Neuropathic Pain

Neuropathic pain arises from peripheral nerve injury or CNS lesion; ectopic discharge, spinal wind-up (NMDA/SP), and central sensitization maintain pain beyond healing. FDA-approved treatments: duloxetine, pregabalin, gabapentin, and topical agents.

Also known as: neuropathic pain, peripheral neuropathy pain, diabetic neuropathy, postherpetic neuralgia, central sensitization pain, allodynia, hyperalgesia, ectopic discharge
