Wilms Tumor

Wilms tumor (nephroblastoma) is the most common pediatric renal malignancy; peak age 3-4 years; favorable histology ~90% with 4-year OS >95%; anaplastic ~10% with TP53 mutations; WT1/CTNNB1/WTX/DROSHA drivers; actinomycin D+vincristine±doxorubicin backbone.

Also known as: Wilms tumor, nephroblastoma, Wilms' tumor, pediatric kidney cancer, childhood renal tumor, nephroblastoma WAGR, Wilms anaplastic
