Reproductive System

Gonads, ducts, and accessory glands governed by the pulsatile HPG axis (kisspeptin→GnRH→LH/FSH→sex steroids) mediating gametogenesis, sexual development, and endocrine modulation of bone, CVD, immunity, and CNS.
