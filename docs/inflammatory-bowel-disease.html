Inflammatory Bowel Disease

Chronic intestinal inflammation: Crohn's disease (transmural, any GI) and ulcerative colitis (mucosal, colon only). TNF-alpha, IL-12/23, and JAK-STAT drive inflammation. Anti-TNF (infliximab), anti-IL-23 (risankizumab), and anti-integrin (vedolizumab) are mainstay biologics.

Also known as: IBD, Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis, UC, CD, inflammatory bowel disease
