Myeloproliferative Neoplasms

Myeloproliferative neoplasms (PV, ET, MF) are driven by JAK2 V617F in >95% of PV and ~50-60% of ET/MF; CALR and MPL mutations account for remaining cases. Ruxolitinib (JAK1/2 inhibitor) is standard for myelofibrosis and PV; alloSCT is curative for high-risk MF.

Also known as: myeloproliferative neoplasms, MPN, polycythemia vera, PV, essential thrombocythemia, ET, myelofibrosis, MF, primary myelofibrosis, JAK2 V617F MPN, Philadelphia-negative MPN
