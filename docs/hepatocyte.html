Hepatocyte

Parenchymal cell of the liver comprising ~80% of liver volume. Executes >500 metabolic functions: gluconeogenesis, lipid/cholesterol metabolism, protein synthesis (albumin, clotting factors), urea cycle, phase I/II drug metabolism (CYP450s), and bile acid synthesis.

Also known as: liver parenchymal cell, hepatic cell
