Alzheimer's Disease

Neurodegenerative disease defined by amyloid-β plaques and tau tangles; progressive memory loss and dementia. APOE4 is the major genetic risk factor. Anti-amyloid antibodies (lecanemab, donanemab) slow early-stage progression; symptomatic treatment with cholinesterase inhibitors.

Also known as: AD, Alzheimer disease, senile dementia, LOAD, late-onset Alzheimer's
