Diarrheal Disease

Syndrome of ≥3 loose/liquid stools per day; second leading cause of death in children <5 globally. ~1.7 billion cases/year; ~443,000 deaths/year (WHO). Dehydration is the main mortality mechanism; ORS + zinc is cornerstone management.

Also known as: diarrhoea, gastroenteritis, infectious diarrhea, acute diarrhea, cholera, dysentery, traveler's diarrhea
