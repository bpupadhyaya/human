Level 07

Interdependence and cross-regulation

At the system scale, organs are not isolated units but nodes in coordinated physiological networks. The cardiovascular system connects to every other system; the nervous system regulates all of them; the immune system monitors all tissue compartments. All 11 systems are present in the atlas — each with its own dedicated page.

Covered in Atlas
Cardiovascular System
Heart + 60,000 miles vasculature  ·  CO=HR×SV  ·  Hemodynamics  ·  Blood pressure regulation
~5 L blood · complete circuit ~60 seconds
Nervous System
CNS + PNS  ·  86B neurons  ·  Somatic + autonomic divisions
~100T synaptic connections · signals at 120 m/s
Immune System
Innate + adaptive  ·  Self vs non-self  ·  Tolerance vs autoimmunity
~2 trillion immune cells · ~5 g antibodies in serum
Respiratory System
Lungs + airways  ·  Gas exchange  ·  Acid-base balance  ·  Ventilation control
~500 mL tidal volume · 6 L/min at rest
Digestive System
GI tract + liver + pancreas  ·  Nutrient absorption  ·  Gut-brain axis
~9 m total length · 24–72 hr transit time
Renal System
Kidneys + ureters + bladder  ·  Fluid/electrolyte balance  ·  Acid-base  ·  RAAS
~180 L filtered/day · 1.5 L urine excreted
Endocrine System
Hypothalamus-pituitary axes + peripheral glands  ·  Hormone signaling  ·  Metabolic regulation
~50+ identified hormones · picogram-level signaling
Musculoskeletal System
Bone + cartilage + muscle + tendons  ·  Locomotion  ·  Calcium homeostasis
~206 bones · ~650 skeletal muscles · 40% body mass
Lymphatic System
Lymph vessels + nodes + spleen + thymus  ·  Fluid balance  ·  Immune trafficking
~600–700 lymph nodes · 2–4 L lymph returned to blood/day
Integumentary System
Skin + hair + nails  ·  Barrier  ·  Thermoregulation  ·  Vitamin D synthesis
~1.7 m² surface area · ~4 kg largest organ by mass
Reproductive System
Gonads + ducts  ·  Gamete production  ·  Sex hormones  ·  HPG axis
~300M sperm/ejaculate · ~400 oocytes released over lifetime
