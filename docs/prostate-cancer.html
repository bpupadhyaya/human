Prostate Cancer

Second leading cancer death in men; driven by androgen receptor (AR) signaling; PTEN loss (~50%) and AR amplification underlie castration-resistant progression. Docetaxel, abiraterone, enzalutamide, olaparib (BRCA-mutant HRR deficient), and lutetium-177-PSMA are active in mCRPC.

Also known as: PCa, prostate adenocarcinoma, CRPC, castration-resistant prostate cancer, mCRPC, hormone-sensitive prostate cancer, mCSPC, CSPC
