Primary CNS Lymphoma

PCNSL is ABC-DLBCL confined to CNS; MYD88 L265P ~90%, CD79B ~70%; high-dose methotrexate is the treatment backbone; ibrutinib shows ~50-60% ORR in R/R disease; 5-year OS ~30-40% with HDMTX-based therapy; vitreous IL-10 >10 pg/mL is pathognomonic.
