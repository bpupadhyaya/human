Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation

DIC is a syndrome of microvascular thrombosis and consumption coagulopathy from systemic thrombin activation; sepsis, obstetric emergencies, and malignancy are top triggers. ISTH score ≥5 = overt DIC. Treatment targets underlying cause; FFP + cryoprecipitate for bleeding.

Also known as: DIC, disseminated intravascular coagulation, consumptive coagulopathy, defibrination syndrome, DIC coagulopathy, overt DIC, non-overt DIC, microangiopathic coagulation
