Synovial Sarcoma

Synovial sarcoma is defined by SS18-SSX1/SSX2 fusion (t(X;18)) → SMARCB1 displacement from BAF → EZH2 dependency; ~800/year USA; biphasic/monophasic histology; TLE1 IHC positive; ifosfamide-based chemotherapy; tazemetostat (SARC057 ORR ~22%) and trabectedin active.

Also known as: synovial sarcoma, SS18-SSX sarcoma, biphasic synovial sarcoma, monophasic synovial sarcoma, t(X;18) sarcoma, TLE1-positive sarcoma, translocation sarcoma SYT-SSX, synovial cell sarcoma
