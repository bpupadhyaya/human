Psoriasis

Chronic skin disease driven by Th17/IL-17 axis, TNF-alpha, and keratinocyte hyperproliferation; thickened scaly erythematous plaques. IL-17 inhibitors (secukinumab, ixekizumab), IL-23 inhibitors (risankizumab), and anti-TNF (adalimumab) provide near-complete skin clearance.

Also known as: plaque psoriasis, psoriasis vulgaris, PsO, PsA, psoriatic arthritis, palmoplantar psoriasis
