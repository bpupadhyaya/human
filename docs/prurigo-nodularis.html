Prurigo Nodularis

Prurigo nodularis is a chronic neuro-inflammatory skin disease with hyperkeratotic nodules driven by an itch-scratch cycle; Th2/Th22 inflammation and IL-31/IL-4/IL-13 signaling; nemolizumab (anti-IL-31RA) and dupilumab (anti-IL-4Rα) are FDA-approved treatments.

Also known as: PN, prurigo nodularis Hyde, nodular prurigo, chronic prurigo, lichen obtusus
