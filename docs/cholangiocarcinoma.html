Cholangiocarcinoma

Cholangiocarcinoma includes intrahepatic (iCCA), perihilar, and distal subtypes; FGFR2 fusions and IDH1 mutations (~15% each) define iCCA. Gemcitabine/cisplatin+durvalumab (TOPAZ-1) is first-line; pemigatinib is approved for FGFR2-fusion iCCA; ivosidenib for IDH1-mutant CCA.

Also known as: cholangiocarcinoma, CCA, intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma, iCCA, perihilar cholangiocarcinoma, Klatskin tumor, biliary tract cancer, BTC, bile duct cancer, FGFR2 fusion CCA
