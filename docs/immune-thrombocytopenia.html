Immune Thrombocytopenia

Immune thrombocytopenia (ITP): anti-platelet IgG (anti-GPIIb/IIIa) → FcγR-mediated splenic destruction + CD8+ T-cell lysis; platelet <100×10⁹/L. Corticosteroids/IVIG first-line; romiplostim, eltrombopag (TPO-RAs); efgartigimod (FcRn inhibitor; FDA Jun 2023).

Also known as: ITP, immune thrombocytopenic purpura, idiopathic thrombocytopenic purpura, primary ITP, anti-platelet antibody
