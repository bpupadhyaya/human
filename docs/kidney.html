Kidney

Paired retroperitoneal organs (~150 g each) housing ~1 million nephrons. Core functions: plasma filtration (GFR ~125 mL/min), tubular reabsorption, acid-base balance, and endocrine roles (EPO, renin, calcitriol). Central to blood pressure regulation via RAAS.

Also known as: ren, renal organ
