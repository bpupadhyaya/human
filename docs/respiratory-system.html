Respiratory system

Airways, lungs, chest wall, and diaphragm forming the gas-exchange apparatus: ~6 L/min ventilation to 300 million alveoli. Coupled to the cardiovascular system via the pulmonary circuit. Controlled by medullary respiratory centres and chemoreceptors.

Also known as: respiratory system, pulmonary system, ventilatory system
