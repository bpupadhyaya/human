BDNF

BDNF activates TrkB → MAPK/PI3K → neuronal survival, dendritic complexity, and LTP; reduced in MDD, Alzheimer's, and with aging; exercise robustly raises BDNF; Val66Met SNP impairs activity-dependent secretion and elevates risk for depression and cognitive decline.

Also known as: brain-derived neurotrophic factor, BDNF, NGF-2, BDNF Val66Met, TrkB ligand, neurotrophin, NTRK2 ligand, BDNF neuroplasticity, hippocampal BDNF
