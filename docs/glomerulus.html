Glomerulus

Tuft of fenestrated capillaries (~8–10 loops) in Bowman's capsule — the nephron's primary filtration unit. Three-layer barrier filters ~180 L/day of plasma at GFR ~125 mL/min. ~1 million glomeruli per adult kidney.

Also known as: renal glomerulus, glomerular tuft, Malpighian corpuscle
