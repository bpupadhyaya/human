HIV gp120

HIV gp120 (surface subunit of Env; ~120 kDa; 27 N-linked glycans) binds CD4 (Phe43 cavity) then CCR5/CXCR4 to trigger gp41 fusion; glycan shield conceals bNAb epitopes; CD4-binding site, V1V2 apex, and MPER are targets of VRC01, PG9, and SOSIP vaccine immunogens.

Also known as: HIV gp120, HIV envelope glycoprotein, HIV Env, gp120, gp160, HIV gp41, broadly neutralizing antibody HIV, bNAb, SOSIP, VRC01, CD4-binding site antibody, HIV vaccine gp120
