Bladder Cancer

Urothelial carcinoma of the bladder; FGFR3 mutations in ~25-35% and TERT promoter mutations in ~75%; PD-L1 expression guides immunotherapy. BCG is standard for non-muscle-invasive disease; enfortumab vedotin + pembrolizumab is first-line for metastatic urothelial carcinoma.

Also known as: urothelial carcinoma, bladder urothelial carcinoma, transitional cell carcinoma, NMIBC, MIBC, metastatic urothelial carcinoma, mUC, bladder TCC
