Tuberculosis

Tuberculosis (MTB; Mycobacterium tuberculosis) causes ~10M cases and 1.3M deaths annually; inhaled droplet nuclei → macrophage phagosome arrest → granuloma; HRZE 6-month regimen for drug-sensitive TB; BPaL (bedaquiline-pretomanid-linezolid) for MDR-TB.

Also known as: TB, pulmonary tuberculosis, Mycobacterium tuberculosis, MTB, MTBC, LTBI, latent TB, MDR-TB, XDR-TB, Pott's disease, phthisis, consumption
