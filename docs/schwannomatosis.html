Schwannomatosis

Schwannomatosis is caused by germline SMARCB1 (~40%) or LZTR1 (~30%) mutations; multiple peripheral schwannomas WITHOUT bilateral vestibular schwannomas; chronic pain is the hallmark; distinct from NF2; treatment: surgical resection for symptomatic tumors.

Also known as: schwannomatosis, multiple schwannomatosis, SMARCB1 schwannomatosis, LZTR1 schwannomatosis, schwannomatosis type 1, schwannomatosis type 2, sporadic schwannomatosis, schwannomatosis NF2-negative, hereditary schwannomatosis
