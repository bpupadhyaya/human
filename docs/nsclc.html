NSCLC

Most common lung cancer subtype (~85%); adenocarcinoma (KRAS/EGFR/ALK drivers), squamous cell carcinoma, and large cell. EGFR TKIs (osimertinib) and ALK inhibitors (alectinib) are highly active; pembrolizumab + chemotherapy transforms KRAS/squamous disease.

Also known as: non-small cell lung cancer, lung adenocarcinoma, squamous cell lung carcinoma, LUAD, LUSC, lung cancer, NSCLC adenocarcinoma
