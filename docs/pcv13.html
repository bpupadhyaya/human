PCV13 — Prevnar 13

Platform: Polysaccharide-protein conjugate (CRM197)  |  Route: IM  |  Manufacturer: Pfizer  |  Schedule: 3+1 (2, 4, 6 months + 12–15 months)

PCV13 contains capsular polysaccharide (CPS) antigens from 13 Streptococcus pneumoniae serotypes (1, 3, 4, 5, 6A, 6B, 7F, 9V, 14, 18C, 19A, 19F, 23F) individually conjugated to CRM197 — a non-toxic G52E diphtheria toxin mutant. Conjugation converts the T-cell-independent polysaccharide antigens to T-cell-dependent, enabling germinal center formation, affinity maturation, memory B-cell generation, and effective immunisation of infants. Replaced PCV7 (2010–2012) in most national programs, adding critical serotypes 3, 6A, and 19A (then dominant post-PCV7 replacement serotype). CAPiTA RCT (n=84,496): 75% VE against vaccine-type IPD in adults ≥65; first RCT demonstrating pneumococcal vaccine efficacy against pneumonia.

Overview

Streptococcus pneumoniae encases itself in a polysaccharide capsule that is the primary virulence factor — resisting phagocytosis — and the principal target of protective antibodies. There are >100 serotypes, defined by capsular polysaccharide chemistry. The 13 serotypes in PCV13 (1, 3, 4, 5, 6A, 6B, 7F, 9V, 14, 18C, 19A, 19F, 23F) accounted for the majority of invasive pneumococcal disease (IPD — bacteraemia, meningitis, bacteraemic pneumonia) in the pre-vaccine era.

The key innovation of PCV13 versus earlier 23-valent polysaccharide vaccine (PPSV23 / Pneumovax) is conjugation to CRM197. Plain polysaccharides are T-cell-independent (TI-2) antigens — they cross-link B-cell receptors without T-cell help, generating limited affinity maturation, no long-lived memory B cells, and completely failing to immunise infants under 2 years (whose TI-2 B-cell signalling is immature). Conjugation to CRM197 recruits carrier-specific CD4&sup+ T-helper cells, enabling germinal center reactions, class-switch recombination, and robust immunological memory from 6 weeks of age.

The addition of serotype 19A over PCV7 was critically important — 19A had expanded dramatically through serotype replacement after PCV7 introduction, becoming the dominant invasive serotype in the US and Europe. PCV13's inclusion of 19A led to a measurable additional reduction in 19A IPD, validating the serotype expansion strategy.

Platform & Conjugation Chemistry

From T-cell-independent to T-cell-dependent — the conjugate vaccine principle

  PLAIN POLYSACCHARIDE (PPSV23 — T-cell INDEPENDENT):
  CPS → direct BCR cross-linking (TI-2)
        │  No T-cell help required
        │  Marginal zone B cells respond
        ▼
  IgM → limited IgG2 class-switch
  No germinal center → no affinity maturation
  No memory B cells → no booster response
  Fails under age 2 → NOT usable in infants

  POLYSACCHARIDE-PROTEIN CONJUGATE (PCV13 — T-cell DEPENDENT):
  CPS–CRM197 conjugate → APC uptake
        │
        ├─ CPS binds BCR → B cell activated
        │
        └─ CRM197 processed via MHC II → CD4⁺ T helper
                    │  CD40L · IL-4 · IL-21 costimulation
                    ▼
           Germinal center reaction
                    │  Affinity maturation (somatic hypermutation)
                    │  Class-switch: IgG1 / IgG2
                    ▼
           High-affinity anti-CPS IgG
           Long-lived plasma cells (bone marrow)
           Memory B cells (rapid re-call)
           Effective from 6 weeks of age in infants

  CRM197 CARRIER PROTEIN:
  Non-toxic diphtheria toxin G52E point mutant
  Highly immunogenic (prior diphtheria vaccination primes CRM197-specific T cells)
  Produced in Corynebacterium diphtheriae or Pseudomonas fluorescens
  Same carrier used in Hib-CRM197, MenACWY-CRM (Menveo)
  1. 13 individual conjugates: Each of the 13 pneumococcal CPS serotypes is individually grown from pneumococcal culture, purified, and separately conjugated to CRM197 via reductive amination chemistry. The 13 conjugates are then blended into the final formulation — the most complex conjugate vaccine manufacturing process of any licensed vaccine.
  2. Aluminium phosphate adjuvant: Adsorbed on aluminium phosphate (not aluminium hydroxide); enhances antigen uptake at injection site, activates NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated innate signalling (IL-1β, IL-18), promotes DC maturation and migration to draining lymph nodes.
  3. Opsonophagocytic killing: Anti-CPS IgG opsonises pneumococci, enabling Fc-receptor-mediated phagocytosis by neutrophils and macrophages. The Opsonophagocytic Killing Assay (OPKA) is the functional correlate of protection (titre ≥1:8; anti-CPS IgG ≥0.35 µg/mL by ELISA).
  4. Herd protection: PCV13 prevents nasopharyngeal carriage of vaccine-type pneumococci, interrupting transmission and creating herd protection for unvaccinated elderly and immunocompromised individuals — a critical indirect effect confirmed by US ABCs surveillance.
  5. Infant efficacy: Unlike PPSV23, PCV13 generates protective immunity from 6 weeks of age via the T-cell-dependent mechanism — the primary public health advantage, since the highest-risk period for IPD is the first 2 years of life.

Immunogenicity

Anti-CPS IgG (Primary)

Type-specific anti-capsular polysaccharide IgG is the primary correlate of protection. Threshold: ≥0.35 µg/mL (ELISA) or ≥1:8 OPKA titre. High-affinity IgG1 and IgG2 generated after germinal center reaction; affinity maturation driven by T-cell help from CRM197-primed CD4&sup+ cells.

Opsonophagocytic Function

OPKA titre is the most clinically relevant functional measure — tests whether anti-CPS antibodies enable neutrophil-mediated killing of pneumococci in vitro. Higher OPKA titres correlate with protection against IPD. PCV13 OPKA responses exceed PPSV23 for all 7 shared serotypes in infants; similar in adults.

Memory B Cells & Durability

Germinal center-derived memory B cells persist for years after PCV13 primary series; robust anamnestic (booster) response on re-exposure or booster dose. Contrast with PPSV23: repeat dosing causes hyporesponsiveness (tolerance), not boosting. PCV13 booster (PCV20 or PPSV23 after PCV13) gives additive coverage without hyporesponsiveness.

T-Cell Responses

CRM197-specific CD4&sup+ T cells are primed by PCV13; these cells provide carrier-mediated T-cell help for anti-CPS B cells. Prior diphtheria vaccination (universal) pre-primes CRM197-specific T cells, potentially enhancing conjugate responses. Antigen-specific pneumococcal T cells contribute to memory maintenance but are not the primary protective correlate.

Safety Profile

  • Very Common Injection-site reactions — pain, redness, swelling in >60% of doses. Alum depot; resolves 24–48 hours. Warm compress as needed. More pronounced in adults than infants.
  • Common Fever, irritability, drowsiness (infants) — low-grade fever (<38.5 °C) in ~25–40% of infant doses; irritability and drowsiness common; resolve within 24–48 hours.
  • Very Rare Febrile seizure — marginally elevated risk (~1:3,000) when co-administered with some inactivated influenza vaccines in children; not observed at elevated rates with standard EPI co-administration (DTaP, Hib, IPV, HepB).
  • Very Rare Anaphylaxis — <1 per million doses. No association with: autism, SIDS, asthma, atopy, Crohn's disease, or autoimmune disorders.
