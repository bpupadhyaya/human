Atherosclerosis

Chronic arterial intimal disease driven by LDL oxidation, macrophage foam cell formation, and smooth muscle migration; vulnerable plaque rupture causes MI and stroke. Statins, PCSK9 inhibitors, and anti-inflammatory therapies (colchicine) are evidence-based interventions.

Also known as: arteriosclerosis, coronary artery disease, CAD, ASCVD, atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease
