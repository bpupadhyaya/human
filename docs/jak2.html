JAK2

JAK2 non-receptor tyrosine kinase mediates EPO, TPO, and cytokine receptor signaling via STAT5/STAT3; JAK2 V617F pseudokinase domain mutation causes constitutive kinase activity in polycythemia vera (>95%), essential thrombocythemia, and myelofibrosis; ruxolitinib is approved.
