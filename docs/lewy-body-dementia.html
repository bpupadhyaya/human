Lewy Body Dementia

DLB (dementia with Lewy bodies) is the 2nd most common neurodegenerative dementia; core features: fluctuating cognition, visual hallucinations, RBD, parkinsonism; cortical alpha-synuclein Lewy body pathology; fatal neuroleptic sensitivity; rivastigmine for cognition.

Also known as: DLB, dementia with Lewy bodies, Lewy body disease, LBD, diffuse Lewy body disease, Lewy body dementia, PDD, Parkinson's disease dementia, synucleinopathy dementia
