Hepatitis B

HBV (hepadnavirus; RC-DNA; 3.2 kb) infects 296M people; replicates via pgRNA/reverse transcriptase; tenofovir/entecavir suppress but do not clear cccDNA; HBeAg seroconversion marks immune control; 50-55% of global HCC; functional cure (HBsAg loss) is emerging treatment goal.
