IL-23

IL-23 is a heterodimeric cytokine (IL-23p19/p40) produced by DCs and macrophages; IL-23 → IL-23R/JAK1/TYK2/STAT3 → Th17 cell maintenance and IL-17 production; IL-23 drives psoriasis and IBD; anti-IL-23p19 antibodies (risankizumab, guselkumab) are first-line biologics.

Also known as: IL-23, interleukin-23, IL-23p19, IL23A, IL-23/IL-17 axis, IL-23 cytokine, anti-IL-23, risankizumab target, Th17 cytokine, IL-23 IBD
