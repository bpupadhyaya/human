Uveal Melanoma

Uveal melanoma is the most common primary intraocular malignancy; GNAQ/GNA11 ~85%, BAP1 ~45%, SF3B1 R625 ~15-20%, EIF1AX ~15%; Class 2 (BAP1 loss) has 25-35% 5-year metastasis-free survival; tebentafusp (gp100×CD3) is FDA-approved for HLA-A*02:01+ metastatic disease.

Also known as: uveal melanoma, choroidal melanoma, iris melanoma, ciliary body melanoma, intraocular melanoma, ocular melanoma, GNAQ melanoma
