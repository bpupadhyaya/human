Binge Eating Disorder

Binge Eating Disorder (2-3% prevalence; most common US eating disorder) characterized by recurrent loss-of-control eating without compensation; driven by dopamine reward dysregulation, serotonin hypofunction, and ghrelin dysregulation. Lisdexamfetamine (Vyvanse) is FDA-approved.

Also known as: binge eating disorder, BED, compulsive overeating, loss of control eating, binge eating, food addiction
