Phospholamban

Phospholamban (PLN, gene PLN) — a 52-amino acid transmembrane micropeptide and the tonic inhibitor of SERCA2a. In its dephosphorylated state PLN reduces SERCA2a Ca²⁺ affinity (Km shifts ~0.2 → 0.6 µM). PKA phosphorylation at Ser16 (downstream of β1-AR) or CaMKII phosphorylation at Thr17 relieves inhibition, producing lusitropy and increased SR Ca²⁺ loading — the molecular relay of sympathetic cardiac reserve.

Gene: PLN · 6q22.31 UniProt: P26678 Size: 52 amino acids, ~6 kDa Scale: 03-Molecular

Overview

Phospholamban (PLN) is a 52-amino acid transmembrane micropeptide expressed almost exclusively in cardiac muscle and slow-twitch skeletal muscle. It functions as the tonic inhibitor of SERCA2a — the SR Ca²⁺-ATPase that removes ~70% of cytosolic Ca²⁺ after each contraction. In its dephosphorylated state, PLN associates with SERCA2a and reduces the pump's apparent Ca²⁺ affinity, shifting its Km from ~0.2 µM (uninhibited) to ~0.6 µM — a threefold shift that significantly reduces pump rate at the low Ca²⁺ concentrations of diastole (~100 nM).

When PLN is phosphorylated at Ser16 (by PKA, downstream of β1-adrenergic stimulation) or at Thr17 (by CaMKII), it dissociates from SERCA2a, restoring full pump activity. Faster Ca²⁺ reuptake simultaneously: (1) lowers diastolic [Ca²⁺] faster → faster muscle relaxation (lusitropy); and (2) increases SR Ca²⁺ loading → larger systolic Ca²⁺ transient next beat → greater contractility. PLN is thus the molecular relay through which sympathetic activation simultaneously increases contractility and ensures complete relaxation between now-faster beats.

Physiological brake: PLN inhibition is the substrate for β-adrenergic reserve. In PLN-knockout mice, basal contractility is enhanced (SERCA2a is fully active), but the additional catecholamine response is blunted — the heart loses its sympathetic reserve. PLN is not merely a brake; it is the stored potential for catecholamine-driven performance.

Pentameric Storage Form

In resting cardiac SR, PLN protomers exist in equilibrium between: monomers (the active inhibitory species that bind SERCA2a) and a pentamer (the inactive storage form — five PLN protomers form a "pinwheel" structure). PKA phosphorylation of monomers shifts the equilibrium toward phosphorylated monomers, recruiting additional monomers from the pentamer pool and amplifying the regulatory range.

Mechanism

PLN–SERCA2a Inhibition and β-AR Relief Pathway

── Phospholamban regulatory axis ───────────────────────── RESTING STATE (low sympathetic tone): PLN dephosphorylated (~60–80% dephospho at rest) PLN-TM engages SERCA2a TM2/6/9 → stabilizes E2 conformation PLN-Ia contacts SERCA2a actuator domain → Km for Ca²⁺ shifts: 0.2 µM (free) → 0.6 µM (PLN-bound) → SERCA2a pump rate ~50% of maximum at diastolic [Ca²⁺] → Ca²⁺ transient decay τ ~150–200 ms (human ventricle) SYMPATHETIC STIMULATION: NE → β1-AR → Gαs → adenylyl cyclase → ↑cAMP ↓ PKA phosphorylates PLN Ser16 ↓ Negative charge on PLN-Ia → electrostatic repulsion from SERCA2a PLN disengages (or adopts non-inhibitory orientation) ↓ SERCA2a operates at intrinsic Ca²⁺ affinity: Km ~0.2 µM → FASTER Ca²⁺ reuptake → SHORTER decay τ → FASTER relaxation (lusitropy) ✓ → HIGHER SR Ca²⁺ load per cycle → LARGER systolic Ca²⁺ transient next beat (inotropy) ✓ FREQUENCY-DEPENDENT (CaMKII): Higher heart rate → more Ca²⁺ per unit time → CaMKII activated → PLN Thr17 phosphorylated → Additional SERCA2a disinhibition → even more SR loading → Positive force-frequency (Bowditch) effect ✓

Inhibition Mechanism: Two-Component

Unphosphorylated PLN inhibits SERCA2a by: (1) Transmembrane interaction — PLN TM helix packs against SERCA2a TM2/TM6/TM9, stabilizing the low-Ca²⁺-affinity E2 state; (2) Cytoplasmic interaction — PLN domain Ia contacts SERCA2a's actuator domain, impeding the domain movements required for Ca²⁺ binding and translocation. The net kinetic effect is a threefold increase in Km at diastolic [Ca²⁺], reducing pump rate approximately twofold at 100 nM [Ca²⁺], while having negligible effect at systolic [Ca²⁺] (~1 µM) where SERCA2a is saturated regardless.
