Mesothelioma

Malignant mesothelioma arises from pleural/peritoneal mesothelial cells; asbestos exposure causes ~80% with 30-50-year latency; BAP1 (~55%) and NF2 (~40%) loss define molecular subtypes. Nivolumab+ipilimumab (CheckMate 743: OS 18.1 vs 14.1 months) is first-line standard.

Also known as: mesothelioma, malignant pleural mesothelioma, MPM, peritoneal mesothelioma, asbestos cancer, epithelioid mesothelioma, sarcomatoid mesothelioma, biphasic mesothelioma, Krenning mesothelioma
