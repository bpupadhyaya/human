Mantle Cell Lymphoma

Mantle cell lymphoma is aggressive B-cell lymphoma with t(11;14) CCND1-IGH → cyclin D1 overexpression and CDK4/6 → S-phase entry; SOX11+, ATM deletion in ~40%, blastoid variant has TP53 mutations. Ibrutinib/zanubrutinib and venetoclax transformed R/R MCL; CAR-T is approved.

Also known as: mantle cell lymphoma, MCL, t(11;14) lymphoma, CCND1-IGH, cyclin D1 lymphoma, blastoid MCL, leukemic non-nodal MCL
