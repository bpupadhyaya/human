Cervical Cancer

Cervical cancer is HPV-driven in >99% of cases; HPV E6/E7 oncoproteins degrade p53 and inactivate RB → immortalization. Cisplatin + chemoradiation is standard for locally advanced; pembrolizumab + chemotherapy (KEYNOTE-826) is approved for PD-L1+ recurrent/metastatic disease.

Also known as: cervical cancer, cervical carcinoma, HPV cervical cancer, squamous cell carcinoma cervix, cervical adenocarcinoma, FIGO cervical cancer, invasive cervical cancer
