Waldenström Macroglobulinemia

Waldenström macroglobulinemia is lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma producing IgM monoclonal protein; MYD88 L265P in ~90% drives NF-κB and JAK-STAT3. Hyperviscosity, neuropathy, and cryoglobulinemia are hallmarks; ibrutinib and zanubrutinib are approved for MYD88 L265P WM.

Also known as: Waldenström macroglobulinemia, WM, lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma, LPL, IgM monoclonal protein, IgM paraprotein, hyperviscosity syndrome, MYD88 L265P WM
