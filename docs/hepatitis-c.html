Hepatitis C

HCV (Hepacivirus; positive-sense ssRNA; genotypes 1-6) infects 58M people globally; NS3/4A cleaves MAVS → IRF3 not activated → chronicity in 80%; direct-acting antivirals (SOF/VEL, GLE/PIB) achieve >95% cure; cirrhosis → HCC risk 1-5%/year; no vaccine exists.
