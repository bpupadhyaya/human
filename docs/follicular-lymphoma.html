Follicular Lymphoma

Follicular lymphoma is the most common indolent B-cell lymphoma; t(14;18) BCL-2-IGH in ~85% drives apoptosis resistance; EZH2 Y641 in ~25% silences tumor suppressors. Rituximab+bendamustine or R-CHOP are standard; tazemetostat is approved for EZH2-mutant relapsed/refractory FL.

Also known as: follicular lymphoma, FL, indolent NHL, t(14;18) lymphoma, BCL-2-IGH, follicle center lymphoma, grade 1-2 follicular lymphoma
