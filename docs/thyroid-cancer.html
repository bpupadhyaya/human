Thyroid Cancer

Malignant thyroid tumors: papillary (BRAF V600E ~60%, RET/PTC fusions ~20%), follicular, medullary (RET mutation MEN2), and anaplastic; differentiated thyroid cancer treated with RAI and lenvatinib/sorafenib; RET-mutant MTC → selpercatinib; BRAF+ ATC → dabrafenib+trametinib.
