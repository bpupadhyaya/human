Colorectal Cancer

Second leading cause of cancer deaths; driven by APC/Wnt loss → adenoma-carcinoma sequence, then KRAS, SMAD4, and TP53 mutations. EGFR blockade (cetuximab) for RAS-wild-type metastatic disease; KRAS/BRAF V600E inhibitors and pembrolizumab (MSI-H) are molecularly targeted.

Also known as: CRC, colon cancer, rectal cancer, colorectal carcinoma, adenocarcinoma of colon, mCRC, Lynch syndrome, FAP
