Macrophage

Professional phagocyte and innate immune effector. Derived from monocytes; tissue-resident forms include Kupffer cells (liver), microglia (brain), alveolar macrophages (lung). Phagocytoses pathogens and apoptotic cells; secretes IL-6, TNF-α, and ROS upon TLR/NLR activation.

Also known as: tissue macrophage, Kupffer cell, microglia, alveolar macrophage, M1 macrophage, M2 macrophage
