Tuberous Sclerosis Complex

Tuberous sclerosis complex (TSC) is caused by germline TSC1 or TSC2 mutations; mTOR hyperactivation → hamartomas in brain (cortical tubers, SEGA), kidney (angiomyolipoma), lung (LAM), skin; everolimus/sirolimus FDA-approved; epilepsy and intellectual disability are common.

Also known as: TSC, tuberous sclerosis complex, tuberous sclerosis, TSC1 syndrome, TSC2 syndrome, SEGA TSC, angiomyolipoma TSC, LAM TSC, TSC brain, TSC epilepsy
