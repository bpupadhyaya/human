Type 2 Diabetes

Chronic metabolic disease from progressive insulin resistance and β-cell failure. Driven by obesity and inactivity. First-line: metformin (AMPK activation); GLP-1 agonists and SGLT2 inhibitors provide cardiovascular and renal benefit beyond glycemic control.

Also known as: T2DM, type 2 diabetes mellitus, non-insulin-dependent diabetes, NIDDM, adult-onset diabetes
