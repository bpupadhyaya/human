NF-κB

Master transcription factor of inflammation. IKKβ phosphorylates IκBα → proteasomal degradation → p65/p50 nuclear translocation → TNF-α, IL-6, IL-1β, COX-2 transcription. Activated by LPS, TNF-α, IL-1β, oxidative stress, and viral dsRNA.

Also known as: NF-κB, nuclear factor kappa B, NFKB, p65/p50, RelA/p50, NF-kB
