T Helper Cell

CD4+ T lymphocyte that orchestrates adaptive immunity. Activated by pMHC-II on dendritic cells, differentiates into Th1/Th2/Th17/Tfh/Treg subsets. Tfh cells drive germinal center reactions and durable antibody production.

Also known as: CD4+ T cell, CD4 T lymphocyte, T helper lymphocyte, Tfh cell, Th1 cell, Th2 cell, Th17 cell
