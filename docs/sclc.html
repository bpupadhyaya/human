Small Cell Lung Cancer

Small cell lung cancer is a high-grade neuroendocrine carcinoma with near-universal RB1 and TP53 loss; atezolizumab or durvalumab + carboplatin/etoposide is first-line for extensive stage; DLL3-targeting tarlatamab is approved for relapsed disease; 5-year OS <10%.

Also known as: SCLC, small cell lung cancer, small cell carcinoma, limited stage SCLC, extensive stage SCLC, oat cell carcinoma, SCLC-A, neuroendocrine lung cancer, lurbinectedin SCLC, tarlatamab SCLC
