Heart Simulator

This page hosts a lumped-parameter (0D) coupled cardiovascular model: two time-varying elastance ventricles, four diode-like valves, and two Windkessel compartments each for the systemic and pulmonary circulations — a closed loop with six volume state variables integrated in real time via fixed-step RK4. The model is based on the framework of Smith et al. (2004) and produces physiologically faithful pressure-volume waveforms, cardiac output, stroke volume, and ejection fraction under resting healthy conditions. Six built-in scenarios — healthy adult, heart failure with reduced EF, aortic stenosis, hypertension, sinus tachycardia, and sinus bradycardia — can be selected to observe how multi-scale dysfunction at the molecular or cellular level (reduced Emax, elevated valve resistance, altered HR) propagates to organ-level hemodynamics. Use the sliders to explore parameter space continuously; the anatomy panel and plots update at 60 fps.
