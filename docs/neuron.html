Neuron

The electrically excitable cell that is the functional unit of the nervous system. ~86 billion in the adult human brain. Core morphology: soma, dendrites, axon, terminals. Propagates action potentials; integrates synaptic inputs to produce patterned output.

Also known as: nerve cell, neural cell, dopaminergic neuron, glutamatergic neuron, GABAergic neuron, interneuron, motor neuron, sensory neuron
