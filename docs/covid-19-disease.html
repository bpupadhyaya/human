COVID-19 Disease

Multisystem infectious disease caused by SARS-CoV-2; clinical spectrum from asymptomatic to severe ARDS and cytokine storm. Spike protein binds ACE2 for cell entry; hyperinflammation drives severe disease; mRNA vaccines (mRNA-1273, BNT162b2) provide high efficacy.
