Selenoprotein Synthesis — UGA Recoding

  Genomic DNA → Pre-mRNA
      │  UGA codon (normally "stop")
      │  + SECIS element in 3′ UTR (hairpin structure)
      ▼
  SECIS-binding protein 2 (SBP2) + EFSec (Sec-specific elongation factor)
      + Sec-tRNASec (loaded with selenocysteine by SecS)
      ▼
  Ribosomal read-through of UGA → Sec incorporated in selenoprotein
  (requires ~10× more energy and complexity than standard aa incorporation)

  Glutathione Peroxidase Cycle (GPx1 / GPx4):
  2 GSH + H₂O₂  ──GPx1──►  GSSG + 2H₂O
  2 GSH + PLOOH ──GPx4──►  GSSG + PLOH + H₂O  (phospholipid hydroperoxides)
      │
  GSSG ──Glutathione reductase + NADPH──► 2 GSH  (recycled)

GPx4 and Ferroptosis

Ferroptosis is an iron-dependent, non-apoptotic cell death defined by uncontrolled lipid peroxidation of polyunsaturated fatty acid-containing phospholipids (PUFA-PLs). GPx4 is the sole enzyme capable of directly reducing PLOOH in membranes. Inhibition of GPx4 (by RSL3) or depletion of GSH (by erastin inhibiting system Xc⁻ cystine/glutamate antiporter) triggers ferroptosis. GPx4 is a therapeutic target in cancer cells resistant to apoptosis.

Absorption & Metabolism

Organic selenomethionine (~90% absorbed) enters the Met pool and is non-specifically incorporated into all proteins. Selenite (Se⁴⁺) and selenate (Se⁶⁺) are ~50–80% absorbed. SePP1 is the circulating Se transport protein for brain and testes. Selenium is excreted as urinary trimethylselenonium and dimethylselenide (expired breath — characteristic garlic odour in toxicity).

The SELECT trial (2011, N=35,533) found selenomethionine supplementation (200 µg/day) did not prevent prostate cancer and possibly increased risk in men with higher baseline Se. This contradicted earlier observational data, illustrating the difficulty of translating nutrient-disease associations from deficiency populations to replete populations.
