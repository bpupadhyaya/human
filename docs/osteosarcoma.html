Osteosarcoma

Osteosarcoma is the most common primary malignant bone tumor; peak 10-20 years; RB1 biallelic loss ~70-75%, TP53 ~30-40%, MDM2 amplification ~6-8%, CDKN2A deletion ~20-30%; MAP protocol (methotrexate+doxorubicin+cisplatin); localized 5-year OS ~70-75%; metastatic ~20-25%.

Also known as: osteosarcoma, OSA, OS bone, osteogenic sarcoma, high-grade osteosarcoma, MAP protocol bone tumor, pediatric bone cancer, conventional osteosarcoma
