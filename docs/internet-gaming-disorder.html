Internet Gaming Disorder

Internet gaming disorder (ICD-11 6C51; DSM-5 Section III) shares behavioral addiction neurobiology with gambling disorder: VTA-NAcc dopamine, OFC hyperactivation, D2R hypofunction, impaired inhibitory control. Prevalence 2-5% of gamers. No approved pharmacotherapy.

Also known as: internet gaming disorder, IGD, gaming disorder, video game addiction, gaming addiction, compulsive gaming, problematic gaming
