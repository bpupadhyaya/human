Hereditary Breast and Ovarian Cancer

Hereditary breast and ovarian cancer syndrome (HBOC) is caused by germline BRCA1/2, PALB2, ATM, or CHEK2 variants; BRCA1 lifetime breast risk ~70%, ovarian ~44%; PARP inhibitors approved across cancer types; risk-reducing surgery (mastectomy/BSO) is standard.

Also known as: HBOC, hereditary breast and ovarian cancer, BRCA syndrome, BRCA1 BRCA2 syndrome, hereditary breast cancer, germline BRCA, HBOC syndrome, BRCA1 germline, familial breast cancer
