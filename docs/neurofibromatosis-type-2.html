Neurofibromatosis Type 2

Neurofibromatosis type 2 (NF2) is caused by germline NF2 mutations; bilateral vestibular schwannomas are pathognomonic; meningiomas, ependymomas also occur; merlin activates Hippo-YAP1; bevacizumab improves hearing; TEAD inhibitors in clinical trials.

Also known as: NF2, neurofibromatosis type 2, NF2 syndrome, bilateral acoustic neuroma, NF2 vestibular schwannoma, NF2 merlin, NF2 bevacizumab, neurofibromatosis 2, acoustic neuroma hereditary
