Influenza

Influenza A/B (orthomyxovirus; segmented negative-sense RNA) causes seasonal epidemics (H1N1, H3N2) and pandemics (1918 H1N1 killed ~50M; H5N1 mortality 60%); NS1 evades RIG-I/MAVS/STAT1; oseltamivir inhibits neuraminidase; baloxavir targets PA; annual vaccines 40-60% effective.
